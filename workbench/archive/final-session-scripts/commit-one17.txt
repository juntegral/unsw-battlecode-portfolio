A long queen no longer splits down to 2 when exposed (QS_SHED_MAX), profile = one17

The queen's emergency rule (own cell in an enemy's reach, no escape: keep 2
cells, the rest leaves as a child) threw away the first tiebreak for long
queens: 21 such splits from 10 or longer in 474 games, 18 of them lost, 15
while she led the queen race. QS_SHED_MAX limits the rule to queens of that
length or shorter; 0 = off = the same program as before. The profile sets 9
and matches the live bot one17 (submission 21693).
