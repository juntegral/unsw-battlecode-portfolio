Wrap-up: final-week docs, tools and the analysis record that lived only on disk

The contest ended on 2026-10-10. This brings into git what had been kept
out of it since 10-06:

- docs: session state log, handover of 10-09, Discord desk rules, promises.
- tools: weak_watch, sweep_games, sweep_loop and edits to cx.sh, smoke.py,
  pair_report.py.
- data/ (ignored by default, added by hand here): the text part of the
  analysts' work (reports, scripts, tables, briefs), the ladder and top-team
  study scripts and reports, every uploaded bot folder with versions.tsv,
  the notices posted to the team channel and the channel mirror. Replays,
  per-game json, caches and worker logs stay out; replays are attached to
  the release data-2026-10-10 as archives.
- archive/: the last session's scripts and switch lists, the standing rules
  given to the AI sessions (claude-memory, claude-rules).

Keys (docs/api.txt, docs/discord.txt) are not included; the staged files
were scanned for them and for token patterns.
