## What happened (5 Oct, card #31)
A teammate typed `/meow force #31 local tests were already enough`. The card was in `status:building` because main had moved and the loop was rebasing his approved build onto it (about 20 minutes left). The bot answered with a correct but confusing warning ("forcing now may open the candidate from the old-base commit and conflict with …"), the teammate found it unclear, and the bot then told him "don't confirm yet, ping me with force #31 once the build is done". He did not come back to it, so a force he had asked for and justified never happened, and the card sat for hours.
How force works today: the confirmed action is written to `<loop out>/meow-requests/<id>.json` by `spool()` in `tools/meow_chat.py`; `controls()` in `work/loop/meow_control.py` applies it when the AI and game locks are free. A forced `set_state` carries `_state` (the state at confirmation) and is refused with "Card moved after confirmation; request a fresh preview" if the state has changed; for `candidate` it also needs `meta['commit']`.

## Wanted
1. Loop side: a forced `set_state` to `candidate` may carry `after_build: true` and `attempt: <n>`. Such a request is not applied and not refused while the card is `building` (or in its mechanism/look steps of that same attempt); it is applied as soon as that attempt has a built commit on the current base, that is when the card reaches `needs-look` (or later states of the same attempt), and the `_state` check accepts that progression. It is still refused, with a plain message, if the build fails, if the attempt number changed (someone asked for a revision), or after 6 hours. Everything else about `controls()` stays as it is, including the audit comment on the card.
2. Bot side: when a member asks to force a card that is `building`, the plan the bot proposes is that deferred request, and the bot says so in plain words, with no talk of conflicts: for example `Force for #31 queen-hunt: skip the remaining tests and open the candidate. The loop is still rebuilding it on the new main (done about 19:11 KST); confirm now and it happens by itself when that build is finished. You don't need to come back.` After the two confirmations the bot's message says the same, and when the runner has applied it the bot posts the result where the request was made (as it does for other queued requests).
3. Also in the bot's instructions (the standing prompt in `tools/meow_chat.py`): do not send a person away to "ask again later" when the bot can queue the action to happen later itself; and when a warning is about timing, say what will happen and when, not what could go wrong.
4. No change to who may force, to the two confirmations, to the reason line, or to what is never forceable.
