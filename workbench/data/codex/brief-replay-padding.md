## What is wrong (already diagnosed; scripts in `~/.claude/jobs/e3d433df/tmp/replay-debug/`, read `fix_replay_padding.py`, `edge_check.py` and `capnp_unpack.py`)
The map text inside a replay numbers edges on a grid whose rows have W+1 slots. Even rows are horizontal edges with W real slots plus one padding slot (`id % (W+1) == W`); odd rows are vertical edges with W+1 real slots. The site's viewer rejects any `EDGE` line on a horizontal padding slot ("edge id 44 is a horizontal padding slot"). Official maps list only real edges. Our generator `work/mkgen.py` (about lines 208 to 217) writes every one of the (2H+1)(W+1) slots, padding included, as empty edges `EDGE i 0 -1`; the engine accepts and echoes them into the replay; `transpose()` in `work/loop/jobs.py` copies that format. So every replay on a generated map (`gen*`, `sq_*`, `sqheld_*`, `fresh_gen_*` and their `_T` copies) fails to open, while official-map replays open.
