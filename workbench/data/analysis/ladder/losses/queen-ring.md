# A queen that cannot be killed: design study for our bot (team 1119, 7 Oct 2026)

**Worth building now:** only the small version, and only in a round the plan leaves free after its changes 1 to 3. By itself it turns about 3 of our 209 lost main games; the large prize (87 losses) needs a safe way to length 23 that no number here supports.
**The design in one sentence:** one default-off switch, QUEEN_RING: when our queen is already 23 or longer, sees no enemy and stands on an open 6 by 4 block whose four turn-end free tiles never spawn, she sheds to exactly 23, walks the fixed 24-tile cycle 6 steps a turn and stops being the feed target; splitting, eating and the feeding rounds are not touched.
**Biggest risk:** a build round goes to a formation our queen can enter in at most one full game in nine (she is 23 or longer by round 400 in 24 of 214); and a queen held at 23 loses the queen rule to any living longer queen (38 of 277 full games end with theirs at 23 or more), so the build leans on knowing their queen is dead, which nobody has measured.

## 1. Geometry
- [C rules/movement.md, execution-order.md, death.md] A dragon must move at least one step or split every turn; its first ceil(L / 4) steps are free and it may take fewer; its own tail tile is blocked for the step; it dies only when a head enters its head tile, when its own head enters something, or when it gives no valid action. So an enemy needs a free tile next to her head that it can reach from open water.
- [S ring_sim.py part A] On the red-team cycle (red:105-106) a body of 23 leaves one free tile, the one ahead of the head. At cycle positions 5, 11, 17 and 23 the head and that free tile are both among the 8 inner tiles of the block, so their six other neighbours are her own body. ceil(23 / 4) = 6 and those four stops are 6 apart. Walked tile by tile with a flood test from the head: 72 turn ends, 0 exposed. From any of the other five starting positions: 72 of 72 exposed. With 1 to 5 steps a turn: 12 to 20 of 24 exposed. There is no slack: exactly 6 steps, every turn.
- [S part A] Same cycle, length 22 or 21: no sealed stop at all. [C rules/execution-order.md] Length 24: no legal move (the tail tile is blocked), she must split.
- General condition [S]: take a closed path of N tiles (N is even) and a body of L = N - g. A stop is good when the head and the g free tiles ahead of it have no neighbour in open water (kelp counts like body). A sealed loop exists exactly when consecutive good stops are never more than ceil(L / 4) apart. Every pair this test accepted was then walked tile by tile under the engine rules with the flood test: 0 exposed turn ends in all of them.
- Pairs in open water [S part B: all Hamiltonian cycles of every block up to 36 tiles; cycle counts match the known ones]: 6x4 with 23 (1 of 37 cycles; step exactly 6), 7x4 with 27 (2 of 92; exactly 7), 6x5 with 29 (4 of 154; longest step 6 of 8 free), 8x4 with 31 (15 of 236; 6 of 8), 9x4 with 35 (54 of 596; 7 of 9), 12x3 with 35 (6 of 32; 9 of 9), 6x6 with 35 (108 of 1,072; 6 of 9). 6x6 also holds at 34 and 33 (2 cycles each): the only block up to 36 tiles that forgives a missing segment. Nothing on 4x3, 6x3, 8x3, 10x3, 4x4, 5x4.
- Smallest [S part D: every closed path of any shape, not only blocks]: up to 22 tiles there are 550,354 paths (per-length counts equal the published numbers of self-avoiding polygons) and none has a sealed loop at N-1, N-2 or N-3. At 24 tiles (2,521,270 paths) exactly two shapes have one, both with 23: the 6 by 4 block (one cycle, and its quarter turn) and a 5 by 5 square walked around its own centre tile, which stays empty water inside her body (2 cycles). So 23 on 24 tiles is the smallest sealed queen in open water.
- With kelp [S part C]: against one straight kelp line or in a kelp corner the smallest is still 23 (8x3, 6x4, more cycles). Between two parallel kelp lines: a 7x2 block with 13 (a corridor two tiles wide), 4x4 with 15, 6x3 with 17; a 6x4 block filling a corridor four tiles high is sealed on all 37 cycles with steps of 4 (2 spare), and one cycle also holds at 22 and at 21. The test in the bot is the same one ("every neighbour across an open edge is my body"), so kelp shapes need no extra rule, only a wider search.
- Replays [M ~/meow-redteam/verify/check.md; M ring_funnel.py]: in both sealed games she is 23 at r186 or r56 and at r200, r300, r400, r500; no enemy head was next to her head in 38,769 and 54,764 turn starts with 45 to 64 enemy dragons alive. My own round-end seal test is first true at r188 and r57 and true in 313 and 444 rounds.

## 2. Getting there in our bot
- Our queen today [M ring_ladder.py]: alive in 250 free games at r100, 124 at r200, 78 at r300. Of the living she is 2 or 3 long in 244 of 250 at r100 and 121 of 124 at r200; the longest at r200 is 13. She eats 5.64 pearls per 100 rounds alive (4,083 pearls in 72,390 rounds, one per 17.7 rounds) and splits 3.31 times a game. She dies in 303 of 386 games: 125 by r100, 235 by r200, 283 of them at length 2 or 3, 196 rammed.
- (a) She stops splitting and eats: no. [C main.cpp:102, 1024] She already stops splitting after round 200, and [M] at r300 she is still 2 or 3 in 41 of 78 and never above 12. At her rate 20 pearls take about 350 rounds [E]. A queen that goes for pearls gets there fast and dies: the red-team ring-first queen reached 22 at r28 to r47 in 6 of 8 games and all 8 died, 6 of them at 22 or 23, unsealed, 9 to 95 rounds after reaching it [M ring_funnel.py]; in its 34 dev games she died at r19 to r117 in 32 [M report.md:75]. The released red-team bot (grows from r100): queen alive at r100 in 6 of 64 games, 22 reached in 6, sealed in 2 (14 and 139 rounds after reaching 22), the other 4 dead 12 to 95 rounds after reaching it [M ring_funnel.py].
- (b) Feeders feed her: this exists, late. [C main.cpp:512-526, 1776-1779, 4919-4922] A queen alive at r300 on a layout above 600 cells pulls feed_round down to 300 and gets the leader bonus, so the feeders die beside her. [M ring_ladder.py, ring_ladder2.py] 68 full free games with her alive at r300: 23 or longer at r400 in 24 (median 21.5 of the 50 alive), at the end in 27 of the 39 alive (median 37). About 100 rounds for 20 pearls. Doing it earlier costs the swarm: 20 pearls are about 40 segments of dead feeders (a dead dragon gives back ceil(L / 2)), against a median team length of 62 at r150 and 73 at r200; this is the code where plan.md fault B lives; and on layouts above 1200 cells she is alive at r200 in only 18 of 79 games. Verdict: use the feeding that already happens at r300, add none.
- (c) Found in the code and the data: QUEEN_KEEP (main.cpp:142, 1025) holds her length with no new code. Alone it is (a) again, but on a layout whose queen spawns 23 or longer it needs no eating. One of the 19 ladder layouts spawns her 25 long: we split her down to 2 or 3 by r50 in 33 of 33 games, she dies in 30, and she is dead at the end of all 15 full losses there, in 9 of which their queen is dead or under 23 [M ring_spawn.py]. "Start at 23 or more: keep 23" is the one early route with numbers behind it; it gives up about 10 children at spawn on a layout where we now win 17 of 33 [E]. Second step, not the first build.
- When it would matter [M ring_spawn.py, ring_ladder2.py]: long queens rarely die. In all 450 main games ours died at 23 or longer in 9, every one rammed: 7 losses, of which 3 turn by rule with a living queen of 23 (full game, their queen dead), 2 were wipe-outs and 2 had their queen longer. Of the 29 queens alive at r300 and dead at the end of a full free game, 20 died still 2 or 3 long.

## 3. Where
- What the bot knows [C main.cpp]: per tile the round last seen, pearl now, and the spawn countdown, -1 for a tile that never spawns (437, 1436); every edge as open, kelp or portal (986, 3903-3907); her spawn tile queen_home (502-503); whether her home area is closed (505-511, 3893); first and last enemy contact (512-521); recent enemy sightings with their age (3660-3672).
- Finding [C red:513-553]: the view is 7 by 7, so a block is fully visible only when her head is on it. Test the 192 placements (8 mirrors and turns by 24 cycle tiles) that put her head on a cycle tile: all 24 tiles seen this round, no dragon on them, every step of the cycle across an open edge (no kelp, no portal), no pearl lying on them, and the four turn-end free tiles never-spawn (all 24 preferred). Only those four are ever empty at a round start, and pearls spawn only then on empty tiles [C rules/pearls.md], so the other 20 may be spawn tiles.
- Getting to one [C red:479-494]: mark the remembered tiles that sit in a clean block and walk to the nearest by path distance. For us: prefer the home side, and skip blocks within 8 tiles of the last contact [E].
- Do such blocks exist [M ring_blocks.py; spawn tiles = tiles with a natural birth in any observed game, a lower bound]: an open 6 by 4 block with no spawn tile exists on 16 of the 19 ladder layouts; with only the four free tiles clean on 17; one starts within 0 or 1 path steps of the queen's spawn head on 13. None on 3: two layouts where almost every tile spawns (2 and 13 percent never-spawn) and one of 288 tiles with no open block.

## 4. Entering and holding
- Start [C red:513-553; main.cpp:481-484, 1389-1399, 3660]: only with no enemy head in view and no sighting within 6 tiles, her body order known, and length exactly 23. Longer: split off length - 23 first when that is 2 or more [C rules/splitting.md]; at 24 take one more pearl, then shed 2.
- The path [C red:537-546]: choose the placement in which a simulated lap from her present tile never meets her own trailing body (it drains one tile a step). Then only walk forward along the cycle. After 23 steps the whole body is on it (4 turns at best); the first turn end on position 5, 11, 17 or 23 with the body on the cycle is sealed, and from then it is 6 steps a turn. She may take fewer than 6 on the way in, so any entry tile works.
- Not yet sealed [C red:555-576; main.cpp:3355-3358]: each turn she ends on the farthest cycle tile within her free steps that no visible head can enter this round (their free plus paid reach, through free tiles). The red-team bot is stricter (it lands within one tile of where it started), which is why its closing took 14 rounds or more.
- A block tile is taken or not in view [C red:561-563, 603]: stop before it; with no landing left, drop the ring state and fall through to the normal queen logic; the finder runs again on the next quiet turn. If the dragon in the way is ours, plan.md change 3 (QUEEN_GIVE_WAY) is the rule that moves it.
- Enemy in view while closing: no new start; a started walk goes on only onto landings that are sealed or out of reach this round, otherwise she hands back to the existing safety rules. Once sealed she ignores heads in view: the ring step returns before the threat code.
- A pearl on her path: with never-spawn free tiles none can appear, since dead dragons drop pearls on their own tiles, outside the block [C rules/death.md]. If she is 24 anyway she has no move and must shed 2 (red:578-582); the child is boxed in and dies, she is 22 for a round or two, which is not sealed on this block [S], then 23 again from the dropped pearl [E].
- Breaking out and re-forming [C red:510]: the state is 24 tiles and a position; it is cleared whenever her head is not where it should be. Paid steps cost segments; under 23 she re-forms only after being fed back up, then sheds.

## 5. Costs
- Early splits: none in the small build (it acts only at 23 or more). An early-growth version would take away her 3.31 splits a game, 3.89 on 2-dragon starts, where fault A of plan.md already costs 56 losses, and 20 pearls: 26 percent of the team's median intake by r100 (76), most of it in the lowest quarter of starts (33 or fewer) [M ring_ladder.py, ring_ladder2.py].
- Their queen alive and longer: we lose on the queen rule. Their queen at the end of our 277 full games: dead 166, alive under 23 in 73, at 23 in 2, longer in 36 (longest 94); against opponents rated 1900 to 2099 she is 23 or more in 23 of 126 [M ring_ladder.py]. In the 23 full free games with both queens alive ours is the longer in 15, and in 3 of those theirs is 23 or more: held at 23, those wins go [M ring_ladder2.py]. So the build rings only once their queen is known dead (main.cpp:1958-1960) [E]; until then she grows as now. Larger blocks (29, 31, 35) are the same code with another table.
- Feeding phase: it conflicts, one line fixes it. From r300 she is the fed leader and feeders die beside her (main.cpp:4924-4935); in the ring she cannot fetch those pearls, and the leader pearl zone keeps the others off them (2339-2354). A queen at exactly ring length must therefore lose the leader bonus (1776); the feed then goes to the longest other dragon, which is also our cover on the next rule.
- Upper bound if a living 23 were free [M ring_ladder.py]: 87 lost full games had their queen dead or under 23 (both dead 59, only theirs 24, both alive 4); our queen was alive at r100 in 62 of them and at r200 in 27. 32 lost full games had theirs at 23 or more and 67 losses ended before round 500: not reached.

## 6. Smallest useful first build
- One constant, QUEEN_RING = 0 (0 = off, 23 = on). Three parts: (1) the 24-offset table and the stop test; (2) one function called where QUEEN_BRAIN is hooked (main.cpp:539) holding finder, walker and shed, gated on "their queen known dead"; (3) one condition in leader_priority (1776). Left out: any growth, split or feed-round change, travel to a remembered block, larger blocks, kelp shapes, the long-spawn keep.
- Mechanism check from replays (the seal test is in ring_funnel.py): local games to round 500 on layouts above 600 cells. A game is eligible when she is 23 or longer at a turn start after the gate opens with no enemy head within 3 tiles. Pass: sealed at a round end within 30 rounds in at least 8 of every 10 eligible games; from the first sealed round to r500 she is alive in every one, 23 at every round end, with 0 round ends that have an enemy head next to her head; with the switch off the games are identical to the base build. About 1 game in 15 is eligible [E: she reaches 23 in 24 of 214 full games and their queen is dead at the end of 6 in 10], so 150 games for about 10; a layout with a long queen spawn makes every game eligible.

## The card

title: Seal a queen that is already 23 long inside her own body (default-off QUEEN_RING)
slug: queen ring
What we tried: Nothing built. Simulated the formation the red-team run found, counted our queen's length, eating and death in 450 main ladder games and the red-team queen's path in 72 of its games, read both bots; the proposal is below.
Did it work: Not run yet. The formation itself held in 4 of 4 games in which it was closed (measured by the red team, 2 re-checked from replays); reaching it is the open part.

### Area
The queen, from round 300 on (the feeding phase), on layouts above 600 cells; plus the leader choice of every dragon for one condition.

### Claim
Estimate, from the simulation and the replays: a queen of 23 walking the 24-tile cycle 6 steps a turn cannot be reached by any head, by the rules and not by a weakness of one bot. Proposal: QUEEN_RING (0 = off, 23). When their queen is known dead and ours is 23 or longer with no enemy in view on an open 6 by 4 block whose four turn-end free tiles never spawn, she splits off the excess, walks onto the cycle and then takes exactly 6 steps a turn; at exactly 23 she no longer counts as leader. No layout name, no change before she is 23.

### Evidence
- Simulated: the 6 by 4 block with 23 is sealed at every turn end (72 of 72) and at no other phase, step count or length; no closed path of 22 tiles or fewer has such a loop (ring_sim.py).
- Measured: closed in 4 red-team games, all won "longer queen, 23 to 0", held 314 and 444 rounds with 45 to 64 of our dragons alive and never a head next to hers (report.md, verify/check.md, ring_funnel.py).
- Measured: their queen is dead or under 23 at the end of 239 of our 277 full games; ours is dead in 204. Ours is 23 or longer by r400 in 24 of 214 full free games; she died at 23 or more in 9 of 450 games, all rammed, 3 of them losses that a living queen turns by rule (ring_spawn.py).
- Measured: a clean block exists on 16 of 19 ladder layouts (ring_blocks.py).

### Expected signal
Estimate. Local, switch on: the mechanism check of section 6 passes (8 of 10 eligible games sealed within 30 rounds; alive, 23 and untouched to r500). Ladder afterwards: full games ending with only our queen alive up from 18 of 277 by a few; no rise in losses with both queens alive (7 of 23 full free games now). In games, about 1 percent: this build proves the holding half, not the growing half.

### What would show it's wrong
- She is eligible and not sealed within 30 rounds in more than 2 of 10 games: the entry (visibility, our own dragons on the block, her trailing body) fails in our bot.
- A sealed queen dies, changes length, or has an enemy head next to her head at any round end: the count or the spawn-tile test is wrong.
- Feeders keep dying within 3 tiles of the ring: the leader condition does not reach the other dragons.
- Queen-rule losses with both queens alive rise: the gate (their queen known dead) opens when she is alive.

## Against it
- Reach [M]: the small build only acts on a queen who is already 23, and such queens hardly die: 9 deaths in 450 games, 3 losses turned. The 283 deaths at length 2 or 3 are untouched.
- No safe way to 23 has been shown by anyone [M]: sealed in 2 of 64, 2 of 34 and 0 of 8 red-team games by a bot written for it; a queen at 22 or 23 and not yet sealed died in 10 of 12 cases. Our queen is 2 or 3 long in 244 of 250 at r100.
- No slack [S]: exactly 6 steps every turn, one pearl or one paid step breaks the count, 22 is never sealed. The forgiving block (6 by 6) needs 33 to 35.
- Held at 23 she loses to any living longer queen [M]: 38 of 277 full games now; the gate depends on knowing their queen is dead, which I did not measure.
- Unseen maps [M]: 3 of 19 known layouts have no usable block; the qualifier layouts are unknown, and best of 7 leaves no room for a build whose effect is about 1 percent.
- The hours [M plan.md]: its changes 1 to 4 each reach more losses (upper bounds 59, 21, about 10 and 35) and come first; the last build starts by 8 Oct 17:30; and this card touches the same queen, leader and feeding code as changes 1 and 3, so it cannot be tested beside them.
- The evidence is 4 closed formations, all against our own bot, never in the sandbox with its CPU limit [M report.md:83-87].
- Not checked: that the bot's knowledge of a dead enemy queen is reliable; that a long-spawn queen can keep 23 and still leave her coil; how often a feeder of ours would stand on the block.
