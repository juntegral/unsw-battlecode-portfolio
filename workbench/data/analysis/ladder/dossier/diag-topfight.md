# How the top teams fight: the same price as ours and twice the starts; three chances their short dragons take and ours refuse (team 1119; 307 fresh top-team games, 469 older, 150 of ours of which 30 against larp and Cutlery; written 8 Oct 2026)
1. **The finding.** [M] A head-on costs every team the same: the attacker dies every time (55,331 of 55,331), and 5 rounds later its side is down 0.8 to 1.3 length (ours 1.1 to 1.5) and the target's side 2.4 to 3.0 (2.9 to 3.1); rate 0.31 to 0.51 for them, 0.36 to 0.48 for us. They start more: 2.5 to 3.8 per 1,000 dragon turns (aAah 1.8, with under half our contact) against our 1.2 to 2.1, and in our 30 games against larp and Cutlery they start 44 a game and we 20. Their dragons of 2 to 4 take three chances our rule refuses: a 3 on a 2 when not outnumbered (0.44 to 0.77 in all seven teams, ours 0.03), a lunge whose extra step is paid by a pearl on the way (0.57 to 0.67 for a 2 in aAah, chad gdp, Cutlery and SSS, 0.27 and 0.42 in two more, never by larp or by us: 0 of 889 turns), and an equal head when the heads in view are level (0.36 to 0.61, ours 0.03). They do not defend better: per contact they step into lunge range as often as ours and leave it less; they end the contact by ramming first. Nobody hunts in groups, walks far for the queen, rams to feed a leader or screens a long dragon.
2. **The change.** Two switches, both off by default. LUNGE_PEARL: in the two lunge searches a pearl seen this round on the way uses up no reach (it pays for its own step). TRADE_SHORT_BY 1: a head showing one part fewer than our length is worth a trade (a 3 on a 2, a 4 on a 3); every other clause of the trade rule stays.
3. **How sure.** Sure of the rules: the fresh games, the older games and the same games seen from both sides agree, and where our rule says yes ours and theirs take the same share (0.93 to 0.95 against 0.77 to 0.89). Not sure they change results: [E] at their rates the two switches add about 10 head-ons a game and about +15 net length, where the head-on account against larp and Cutlery is 28 length a game against us (131 to 102) and their total length at r300 is 218 and 246 against our 99. About one in five that a 30-game test shows it in wins; the mechanism check shows it in one test.
