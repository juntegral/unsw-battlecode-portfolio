# Their queens outlive ours: how the two opponents' queens live, and what of it we can copy (team 1119; 8 Oct 2026)
Sample: today's 150 online games against Big Bobs Bots ("Bob") and WeHaveQuizzes ("Quiz"), 15 layouts each per label: MAIN = `agg7-m1` + `agg7-m2` (60 games, 14 won), B7 = `agg7-b1` + `agg7-b2` (60, 23), B7Q = `agg8-q1` (30, 14; the live bot). `agg7-m3` and `agg8-q2` had not arrived. Every turn of both queens: 25,908 of ours, 45,472 of theirs; the same scan on our 500 ladder games and rank-1's 597. **M** = measured (`work/tq_*.py`), **C** = read from `data/online/smoke/agg8-bot/main.cpp` (line), **E** = estimate. Every number in a table is M.
Terms: queen = lowest starting id of a side. "Within 3 steps" = path over open edges and portals, bodies ignored, the enemy queen not counted; "tiles" = torus Manhattan. R = min(8, (width + height) / 8) tiles from her start tile (4 on 24 by 12, 6 on 32 by 16, 8 from 32 by 32 up). Open-start = her kelp-bounded start region is at least half the board (130 of 150 online games; in the other 20 neither queen can leave). "Walked out" = she died more than R+2 tiles out and no enemy head was within 5 steps on the turn she last left R or the three before; "pushed out" = one was.
## 1. Three lines
1. **Finding.** Their queens do not dodge better and are not longer early: with an enemy head within 3 steps they step away or sprint on 81-89% of turns (ours 87-89%) and are rammed before their next turn on 3-9% (ours 2-5%); at r100 they are 2.3-2.9 long (ours 2.6-3.2). They meet such turns less often, 1.1-3.0 (Bob) and 2.4-4.2 (Quiz) per 100 turns against our 5.1-7.4, because of where they stand: Bob's queen paces around her start tile (69% of her turns inside R, ours 32%; 14% beyond R+4, ours 44%), and both have 3.0-4.1 own heads within 5 tiles after r100 (ours 1.9). Ours walks out unforced: 77 of her 110 deaths in open-start games came after she had left R with no head near, a median 52 rounds earlier. On the live build the two sides ram each other's queen equally (13 and 13 in 30 games); what is left is her deaths by her own move (9 against 2), all 7 open-start ones beyond R+4. With our queen dead and theirs alive we won 0 of 61.
2. **Change.** One switch, `QUEEN_HOME_LEASH` (0 = off, test 8): her step score loses 150 for every tile beyond min(8, (width + height) / 8) from the tile she started on; six lines. No second card.
3. **How sure.** That the habit and the gap exist: high (fates equal `online/agg7.md` and `diag-queenkill.md` count for count). That a leash removes deaths and does not just move them home: low to medium. Far out (beyond R+2) she dies 3.5 times as often per turn as near; 2.0 times within layout, opponent and 50-round band; 1.0 and 0.8 times when her place 10 and 20 of her own turns earlier is used (ladder, 351 deaths: 2.4, 1.9, 1.6). Wins did not move with her early place in any sample. E: between none and a third of her deaths; it is carded because it is the only habit that reaches most of them and one label can refute it by counting turns.

## 3. Why, in the code
- **C.** Nothing in her step score knows where she started. `queen_home` is set on her first turn (522-524) and read once, in the home term at 5215-5217, which sits behind `QG_HOME_RADIUS` 0 (186) and `queen_guard()`, itself behind `QUEEN_GUARD_CELLS` 0 (178, 3415-3417). Her score is the plain dragon's `score_step()` (5039-5248): room and territory at 1.5 a tile (5059, 351-352); the best of pearl 2 x (260 - 40 a step), known spawn tile 1.2 x and unseen 0.2 x the same (5105-5120, 3335-3341, 362-365, 373, 394); 2 a step toward the nearest of those up to 80 steps away (5124-5133, 274-275); 300 for a pearl on the tile (5144-5145); and for any dragon up to 4 long, the queen included, 40 a tile toward the centre when no pearl pulls, in the first 30 rounds on boards up to 700 cells and the first 60 on long boards up to 1,000 (5161-5165, 374-380, 494-496). Her own terms are about threats and room, none about place: the head-race penalty times 4 (5169-5176), dead ends only on boards over 600 cells or a closed home (5083-5092, 3411-3413), the ring veto (5410-5436). `stride_value()` (800-808) scores sprint ends with the same function.
- **M.** So she drifts. Of her 7,375 outward steps beyond R (open-start games), 404 ate a pearl, 931 had a pearl within 3 steps, 2,235 one within 4 to 8 and 3,805 (52%) none within 8. She left R 558 times with no enemy head within 5 steps and 44 times with one.
- **M.** 1421769 (48 by 24, R 8, her start (43,8), `tq_view.py`): r10 (37,10) 8 tiles out, r20 (32,11) 14, r30 (26,9) 18, r40 (21,14) 28, r50 (20,19) 34, no enemy head within 5 steps at any of those rounds; r83 she dies on kelp at (31,15), kelp on three sides, 19 tiles out. ## 4. The card
title: The queen is leashed to the tile she started on (QUEEN_HOME_LEASH 8)
slug: queen-home-leash
### Claim
Our queen leaves her start area unforced and dies out there; Bob's queen, in the same games, paces within a few tiles of hers and is alive at the end three times as often (32 of 65 open-start games against 20 of 130). Proposal: `QUEEN_HOME_LEASH` (0 = as now, test 8) and `QUEEN_HOME_SLOPE` 150.0; in `score_step()` the real queen (3803) pays the slope for every tile of torus Manhattan distance between the destination and `queen_home` beyond min(leash, (width + height) / 8). C: one step outward costs 150, more than the pearl pull of 80 a step and the centre pull of 40, less than a pearl on the tile itself (300 and the full pearl bonus of 520), any threatened tile (900 and up, 5173-5174), a dead end (6,000) or a ring (2e8, 3e8), so she still eats what lies at the edge and still runs from heads. Every map, every round; no map name, no new state.
### Evidence
- M: 77 of her 110 deaths in open-start games are walked-out deaths (MAIN 30 of 45, B7 33 of 45, B7Q 14 of 20), a median 52 rounds after she left R; 56 of those games were lost, 29 on queen length. On the live build 16 of her 20 open-start deaths lie beyond R+4 (9 of 13 rams, 7 of 7 own-move deaths) on 49% of her turns.
- M: per 1000 turns inside R / beyond R+4 she is rammed 1.2 / 6.2 times and dies on her own move 0.6 / 3.3 times; the same slope shows for Bob's queen (0.6 / 2.8), Quiz's (0.4 / 4.2), on our ladder games (1.0 / 4.0), for rank-1 (0.7 / 3.8) and on boards of every size class. Bob's queen spends 14% of her turns beyond R+4 and is alive at the end in 32 of 65 open-start games; ours 44% and 20 of 130.
- M, cost: the term is non-zero on 68% of her turns today (74% on the live build). She eats 771 of her 1,164 pearls beyond R+1, but per turn no faster there (6.2 per 100 beyond R+4, 5.4 inside R; in r0-50 7.1 against 4.5). E: under 1 pearl a game, mostly early ones.
### Expected signal
E, on one label of the new build (`tq_scan.py`, then `tq_tab.py B`): her turns beyond R+4 from 49% to under 20%; tiles from start at r50 and r100 from 12.9 and 12.6 to under 8; enemy heads within 3 steps per 100 turns from 7.4 to under 4; own-move deaths from 9 per 30 games to 4 or fewer; queen alive at the end of r150 from 11 of 30 to 15 or more; pearls a game not under 9 (now 11.1).
### What would show it's wrong
- Her turns beyond R+4 stay above 30%: the term is outvoted (room and territory at 5059, a route at 5438); raise the slope before judging anything else.
- Inside R in r51-150 she is rammed at today's far rate (8 or more per 1000 turns; now 2.5 over the three arms, 4 rams in 1,574 turns): the hunters followed her home and the deaths only moved.
- Own-move deaths inside R above 2 per 1000 turns (now 0.6), her splits by r100 under 2 a game (now 3.0), or own dragons alive at r100 down by more than 2: home is crowded or her walk fed the early economy.
- Games lost with our queen dead and theirs alive not under 10 per 30 (now 10).

## 5. Against it
- **It may move the deaths, not remove them.** The per-turn gap shrinks under every control: 3.5 crude, 2.0 stratified, 1.0 and 0.8 with her place 10 and 20 turns earlier (ladder 2.4, 1.9, 1.6); a chase carries her outward before the kill, and that share of the gap a leash cannot touch. Where her place was her own choice (no head within 5 steps up to r30), the nearer half of games within a layout died by r100 as often as the farther half (12 v 9 online, 29 v 27 on the ladder) and won as often (7 v 8, 85 v 82). That contrast is 5 tiles and both halves walk out later, so it does not test a leash, but it does not support one either. My first cut of it broke ties by outcome and showed a false 6 v 12; the numbers here break ties by game id.
- **Bob's queen still dies at home** (22 of her 33 deaths within R+2, 18 of them rams), with 2.7 own heads within 5 tiles there against our 1.5. A leashed queen is where the enemy expects her, and these opponents took 47 of the 49 sure ways they had.
- **Rank-1 does the opposite and wins** 380 of 597: its queen spends 49% of her turns beyond R+4 and is alive at the end no more often than ours (31% against 25%). It survives by length (4.2 at r100, 23% of turns at 2) and sprints (16% of threatened turns), which is more than a switch.
