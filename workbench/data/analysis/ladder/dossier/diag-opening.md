# Rank-1's opening against ours: level on 15 layouts, ahead on four closed starts, and no switch that earns a build round (team 1119; 500 main ladder games, 597 of rank-1's; written 8 Oct 2026)

1. **What rank-1 does that we do not.** In the opening proper, one rule: its length-4 starters split in round 0 (75.9% of them; ours 4.6%, and 90.3% one round later). Nothing else in rounds 0-20 differs in material: dragons alive 7.6 against our 8.0, total length 19.4 against 19.5, board seen 46% both, spread, steps a turn and parent-to-child distance alike. The two gaps of the brief sit elsewhere. Earlier contact: 6.6 of the 7.4 rounds are three layouts (Tower Defense, the stall; Maze and Trauma, where the sides first see each other across the wrapped border wall), and by path the first meeting is round 23.5 against 26.4. Four-fifths of its lead in total length at round 100 (62.8 against 56.3) is on four layouts whose start is closed off (48.9 against 24.6; the other 15: 66.5 against 64.8); there its farthest head is 24 path steps out at round 30 and ours 13. On the 15 it eats 11 more pearls by round 60 and loses 4 more dragons, for the same length.
2. **The change.** No card. Section 4 writes out two leads for a spare build round: the round-0 split (certain, worth about one dragon-turn a starter) and a no-tail waiver for a short dragon that has not grown for 12 rounds (insurance for an unseen closed start; it would act 0.44 times a game on the known layouts).
3. **How sure.** Sure of the counts: five passes over all 1,097 games, the first equal to the dossier in every game, pearl totals equal by two code paths. Fairly sure the opening is not the lever: inside a layout our lead in length at round 10 goes with winning at +0.01 and at round 20 at +0.12, against +0.25 at round 40 and +0.31 at round 60 (rank-1's own games: -0.01, +0.07, +0.25, +0.35). Not sure of the size of either lead: neither can show on the 19 known layouts, which is why they are not cards.

## 3. Why, in the code (job 3)
- Split. [C] A dragon splits from length 4, always a child of 2 (41, 1034, 1054), if the tile behind the tail is free and the tail has 4 tiles of room (1064, 319), and not while harvesting (1051-1053). harvest_round starts at -1 (5552) and line 744 reads harvest_round == round - 1, which is true in round 0, so every dragon counts as harvesting on its first turn of round 0 and moves; boxed-in starters split by another path (714-720, 748-752), which is the 4.6%. [M] Otherwise the split rules do not hold us back: parent length at the first split 4.0 both, splits by r10 4.5 / 3.8, a length-4 turn is a split in 64% / 68%, rounds held at 4+ before a split 1.38 / 1.36.
- Sprint. [C] Free steps past the first are used only by the stride, which needs two free steps, so length 5+, and never pays (771-781, 3680-3682). Paid steps come only from a ram (2711-2754, traders 3704-3709), a hunt (2632-2675) or an escape (618-650, 765). Nothing pays with nothing in view. [M] So it is: 0.43 paid steps a game, 0.00 per 100 turns with nothing in view; rank-1 2.55 and 0.06. Not a difference worth a rule.
- Centre rule. [C] 40 a tile of wrapped straight distance to the centre for length 4 or less with no pearl or hot bonus, 60 rounds on layouts of 1000 cells or fewer at least twice as wide as high, 30 rounds on others of 700 or fewer (5091-5095, 360-365, 479-481): 9 of the 19. [M] On the six short-start layouts our heads are 12.2 tiles from the centre at r20 and rank-1's 15.0 (op_seq.py). Its cost was Tower Defense; the stall release (368-371, 3097-3105, 5015-5017, 5094) answers that.
- Pearl pull and FAR_GAIN. [C] 2 x (260 - 40 x steps), zero from 6.5 steps (348-350, 3284-3290, 5051-5063); a pearl a visible team mate reaches sooner is not a target (2943-2945, 2962-2970); past that, 2 a step toward the nearest pearl, soon tile, hot tile or unseen tile, to 80 steps (263-264, 5067-5077). [M] Not a deficit: from 8 to 15 steps we eat 43.4% against 41.9%, as fast (third table). With a pearl in view we step nearer as often as rank-1 (pearl 1 step away and no team mate as near: eaten that turn 79% / 82%; 3-4 away: nearer 68% / 70%; op_agg3.py). What differs is where heads are when a pearl is born (within 3 steps 17.4% / 23.3%), which is diag-spawn's subject.
- Unseen bonus. [C] 0.2 x (260 - 40 x steps) toward the nearest tile this dragon has not seen (380, 5058); the memory is per dragon and a child starts blank. [M] Board seen is level. In a closed start it makes each dragon sweep the room (Trauma replay above); that is the turning-back of diag-walk and I add nothing to its no.
- No-tail. [C] 600 off any step from which the survey does not come back to the dragon's own tail, whatever room lies ahead (381, 5016-5019); only the stall release waives it, and that needs the centre rule's layout test and rounds (3097-3100). [M] The fourth table: we are the outlier against rank-1 and against both sets of opponents.
- Queen. [C] The lowest id; queen rules only above 600 cells or in a closed home (3356-3358, 161, 511-517). She splits like any dragon until round 200 (1030-1033, 102, 142), takes a portal only when nothing else is free (144, 3717-3719) and pays 6000 for a dead end (5026-5035). [M] She makes 19% of our splits (rank-1's 10%; 52% against 18% on the four closed starts) and is 2.7 long at r20 (3.3); where she stands does not differ (table 1). Dead by r60: ours 14.7%, rank-1's 22.0%. No lever.

## 4. No card
- Why none. [M] On 15 layouts the opening ends level (length 22.4 / 21.8 at r20, 42.9 / 42.2 at r60) and what rank-1 adds there is turnover that nets 2.6% of length by r100 (66.5 / 64.8, ahead on 8 of 15). [M] A lead at r10 or r20 does not go with winning, in our games or in rank-1's. [M] The four closed starts are four mechanisms, two already answered and two (Trauma, Maze) where we win 66%. [M] In all four columns of the fourth table a one-way step is followed by no more pearls than a loop-back step.
- Lead A, the round-0 split. [C] A switch (SPLIT_ROUND0, default off) that starts harvest_round at -2, nothing else. Mechanism mark for a replay: length-4 starters that split in round 0, now 4.6%, rank-1 75.9%, our opponents 84.8%. [E] Worth one child-turn a starter, 0.1 to 0.3 pearls a game; no result test can show that. Wrong if wall or own-body deaths of rounds 0-5 rise (on the first turn the trail may be unknown and 1059-1061 then splits without the room test).
- Lead B, the idle no-tail waiver. [C] A switch (TR_IDLE_TAIL, default off): at 5016-5017 the penalty is also waived when length is 4 or less, round - last_growth_round is 12 or more (5546, 1385-1388; a child's clock starts at birth) and room ahead is at least TR_STALL_ROOM x length + 8. It is an OR beside the stall release and changes nothing in it. Mechanism mark: the second row of the fourth table, now 29%, rank-1 76%. [M] It would act 0.44 times a game on the known layouts, 4.5 of them a game on Tower Defense, 1.2 on Schooltime #1, under 0.7 elsewhere. [E] Its use is an unseen layout with a closed start outside the centre rule (10 of the 19 known ones are outside), or the rounds after that rule ends: [M] all 174 of our Tower Defense choices fall after round 30 and 2 took the exit; [M, losses/early-growth.md] 18 of 27 games there had no head outside the room by r120, 60 rounds after the centre rule stopped. Wrong if boxed-in deaths (wall, own body, another body) of short dragons rise by more than a tenth, or if a local closed-start layout above 1000 cells shows no earlier exit.
- What would make either a card: a local layout set with closed starts outside the centre rule (lead B), or a build round with nothing better queued (lead A rides along with any change to the split code).
