Terms. Queen = lowest starting id of a side. Steps = path over open edges and portals, bodies ignored; the enemy queen is never counted as an enemy head. Tiles = torus Manhattan. R = min(8, (width + height) / 8) tiles from her start tile; open-start = her kelp-bounded start region is at least half the board. Depth rank = share of her side's other heads that are nearer to an enemy head than she is, every 4th round (0.5 = she stands like any of her dragons, lower = in front of them). Close chase = consecutive turns of hers that start with an enemy head within 3 steps; pursuers = those heads. Boxed in = dead on her own move into kelp or a body, or by an illegal split or no action.

## 4. The card
title: Mates ram a head that is within 5 tiles of our queen (QUEEN_DEFEND_RADIUS 5)
slug: queen-defend-radius
### Claim
A top queen's close chases end after 3 turns because mates are there and ram the pursuer; ours are not sent, so a 2-long head can follow our queen for 20 to 56 turns. Proposal: `QUEEN_DEFEND_RADIUS` 5 (the constant exists and is 0). C: a dragon that sees our queen's head and an enemy head, not their queen, within 5 tiles of it then marks that head as a trade target whatever the lengths and the local count (2727, 2736), walks at it for the trade reward (5244-5245) and rams it inside its ram reach (2748-2791); a mate of 5 to 10 that is not the leader takes it as a hunt target and may sprint up to 8 steps at it, paying length (2661, 2668-2712). The queen herself is unchanged (`!spare`). Every map, every round; no map name, no new state.
### Evidence
