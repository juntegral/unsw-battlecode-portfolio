1. **The finding.** [M] A head-on kills one dragon of each side, so "rammed three times as fast" is half of it. To round 150 the 45 games hold 802 head-ons, 561 started by them and 241 by us; each side loses the same length in them (48 a game us, 49 them), but their side eats 27 of the dropped pearls a game and ours 15, so the account is 33 length a game against us and 22 against them. Of the 526 rams on our non-queen dragons of 2 to 4, 318 follow our own step into the free-plus-one-paid reach of a short head, 100 a turn that began inside it and stayed, 52 a pearl lunge by Cutlery or aAah; the rammer was inside our dragon's 7 by 7 on its last turn in 89%. We step into reach no more often than they do (19.7 times a game; theirs into ours 24.0; top teams among themselves 16 to 26), but after it ours is rammed 36% of the time, theirs by us 14%, top teams among themselves 22 to 27%. Half of our steps into reach (425 of 887) are made by a dragon our rule calls a trader: it is excused the head-threat penalty and paid to walk at its target, so it steps beside the head and the head moves first.
2. **The change.** Two switches, both off by default: TRADER_WARY (a trader is charged the head-threat penalty on its scored step like any short dragon; it still rams whatever is inside its reach) and the existing SMALL_SPRINT_THREAT set true (the same penalty on the tiles a short head reaches by paying). On paper 242 and 135 turns alone, 544 together, and at most 113 of the 526 rams avoided; the two attack switches playing now pre-empt 54 others (2 shared), so defence and first strike add up and neither replaces the other.
3. **How sure.** Sure of the counts: my scanner ends on the replay's own result in 45 of 45 and @@LOK@@ games, the 526 and 215 were recounted with the shared reader (tr_check.py), and our trade rule rebuilt from the board says "ram" on 192 turn starts and ours rammed on 192. Fairly sure the steps change: a non-trader takes a marked tile on 6% of the turns where an unmarked step is open, a trader on 70%. Not sure it shows in results: [E] about 2.5 rams a game avoided and about 3 length a game by r150, against a length gap of 41 to 155; no top team keeps away, so this is a counter to their take rate and not a copy of them.

## Why, in the code
- [C] What SMALL_SPRINT_THREAT marks (map_threats, 2505-2553). For every enemy head in view that shows fewer than 5 parts inside our 7 by 7 (92, 2506), a search from that head over free tiles and our own tail tile (2533; 1531) sets threat_depth_small to 1 on every tile within small_reach steps: 1 with the switch off, max(1, parts - 1) with it on (2507, 2552). So a head showing 2 parts marks its free neighbours as now, 3 parts the tiles within two steps, 4 parts within three: the plain reach ceil(L/4) + L - 2 of a short head that is all in view. Not marked: a tile reached only over a pearl (52 rams), and the ring of a head whose tail is outside the view (a 3 showing 2 parts counts as a 2: 47 rams).
- [C] The charge (score_step, 5177-5190): 450 x max(0.5, L/4), that is 225, 338, 450 for our 2, 3, 4 (25, 28), the same at any depth; a pearl step is worth 300 + 520 (103, 362-364), so the pearl wins. It is not charged when is_trader() (5177): length 4 or less, not the leader, a trade target in view (3759-3764), which needs own heads in view plus one minus enemy heads at least 1 and a head showing our length (2717-2726). The queen already has the hard form: map_hits (3822-3880) marks free and paid reach, counting a body cut by the view edge one longer (3845-3846), and 5410-5436 refuse those tiles while another is open; it runs for the real queen only (1564).

Its scored step comes first (615) and the lunge is tried after it (622) within sprint_reach (2752, 3728-3733), one step for a 2: two tiles from a head it steps beside it and must wait a turn. title: A trader keeps out of the reach of the head it stalks (TRADER_WARY)
slug: trader wary
### Claim
[C] A short dragon with a trade target is excused the head-threat penalty (5177) and paid toward the target (5244), so it steps onto the tile beside the head it cannot yet ram, and that head moves before it does. Proposal: one bool; with it on, the charge of 5178-5190 applies to a trader as to any short dragon. The lunge (ram_path, 622) is untouched and runs after the scored step, so a trader still rams every head inside its reach; the real queen keeps today's branch; with the switch off the build is the same.
### Evidence
[M] 45 games, r0-150: a trader stepped onto a one-step-marked tile with an unmarked step open on 242 turns (5.4 a game); 46 were rammed before their next turn and 2 were followed by our own ram. Counting the paid ring: 409 turns, 69 rammed, 16 followed by our ram. In meetings, when ours is the one to step beside (450) theirs rams in 195 and ours in 30. Our rams do not come from that step: it is the turn before 2 of our 240 rams (16 with the ring).
### Expected signal
[E] tr_scan.py, then tr_why.py and tr_x.py, on any replays of ours: steps into reach by a trader from 9.4 a game (425) to under 4; the row "trader chose the now-marked" from 0.70 to under 0.15; rams on our short dragons a game down by about 1 (11.7 now); our own rams a game (5.3) not down by more than 0.5.
