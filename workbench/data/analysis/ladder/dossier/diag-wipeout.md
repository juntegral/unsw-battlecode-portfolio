# How we get wiped out: a race for the first pearls lost by round 30, then a pick-off (team 1119; 45 online games of current main, 500 ladder games, 597 of rank-1's; written 8 Oct 2026)

1. **How we get wiped out.** The count first: every dragon of ours is dead in 5 of this morning's 30 games, not 11 (7 of 45 with last night's set). The 11 are the games that ended by elimination on either side; in the other 6 it is the opponent that is gone, and those are 6 of our 7 wins. All 7 wipe-outs are on layouts where each side starts with two dragons of total length 4 to 6. We never hold more than 3 to 8 dragons; they eat their second pearl at round 15 and we at round 30 (medians), hold twice our count for good from round 33, and then ram what is left: 59% of our deaths in the 60 rounds before the count halves, at 2 dragons against 25. A race lost in the first 30 rounds and a pick-off after it; not one battle and not a starve. The ladder shows the same (74 of 500, highest count 7 at the median), and rank-1 is wiped out as often as we are (88 of 597).
2. **The change.** No card. No switch reaches the race, and the deaths the brief has me count fall after the game is lost. The one switch that reaches the largest cause among them (SMALL_SPRINT_THREAT, already in the code and off) covers 8% of them on the ladder and 15% online. It is lead 1 of three in section 4, with its one-line diff, because its real size is on layouts above 1200 cells, where nobody is wiped out online.
3. **How sure.** Sure of the anatomy: all 1,142 games replayed by my own state machine with the final state equal to the replay's own in every game, the window tables recounted with the shared reader with no cell different (online 21 of 21, ladder 23 of 23, rank-1 20 of 20), and my ram totals equal to diag-attack's (14,590 and 6,407). Sure that the trade, hunt and split rules are not what loses these games. Not sure why our second pearl comes late where it does: I measured where the two starters walk, not the term that sends them there.
