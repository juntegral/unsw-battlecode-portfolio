# aAah has no growth rule: it stops splitting once it has about 50 dragons, and the dragons of 5 to 8 are what is left (team 1119; 8 Oct 2026, data on disk at 17:15 KST)

## 1. Three lines
1. **Finding.** [M] aAah (179 fresh games, 132 won) splits by three rules, in this order. (1) A head with no free tile beside it splits on 99.6% of turns at any length, round and count under 64; that is 51% of all its splits and the reason only 38% are by a parent of exactly 4. (2) With a pearl beside the head it eats first: a 4-long splits on 16% of such turns (ours 43%). (3) Otherwise the rate depends on its own dragon count and on nothing else I could find: a 4-long splits on 64 of 100 turns under 32 dragons, 45 at 32-43, 34 at 44-47, 18 at 48-51, 7 at 52-55, 2.3 at 56-59 and 0.9 at 60-63, and a longer one the same. Larp, chad gdp and all three of our sets split a 4-long on 40 to 71 of 100 turns right up to 59 dragons and only then stop (1% for us, 7 to 11% for them at 60-63). Under 44 dragons a dragon of aAah that reaches 4 is split within 1.9 turns and 67% never pass 4; from 56 it goes on for 16 turns, two tiles a turn on 65% of them. It gives up nothing in count: 9.5 dragons at r20, 21.6 at r60, 30.4 at r100 against larp's 9.2, 19.2, 29.2 and our 7.7 to 8.3, 15.4 to 16.5, 22.9 to 26.1.
2. **Change.** One card, SPLIT_HOLD_UNITS (default 0 = off): no ordinary split from N own dragons, one constant and one condition; variant A N = 56, variant B N = 48. No second card (eat-first and the boxed-head choice are leads, section 4).
3. **How sure.** Sure of the counts: every game on disk; an independent recount from lengths alone gives the same split rate by count in every cell on 60 games (45 of aAah, 15 of ours). Sure the count is the variable: the same fall on boards up to 700, up to 1,200 and above 1,200 cells, at length 4 and at 5+, for the queen, and in the 19 games to 5 Oct. Not sure it is a threshold: between 44 and 58 it is a rate, so N copies the middle of a slope. Not sure it helps us: larp and chad gdp do not hold, and aAah is already 39% ahead of us in length at r60, before it holds anything.

### Claim
[M] With the head free and no pearl beside it aAah splits a 4-long on 18% of turns at 48-51 own dragons, 7% at 52-55 and 2.3% at 56-59; ours on 52 to 71%, then 1% from 60. [C] The only count test in split_child_size is SPLIT_SOFT_LIMIT 60 (1063). Proposal: add "constexpr int SPLIT_HOLD_UNITS = 0;" and read it at 1063 when it is above 0. Variant A sets 56: the count our own code already calls a full board. Variant B sets 48: where aAah's rate has halved twice. Every map, no round, no new state; escape, cornered and queen-wait splits are untouched. At 0 the build is the same.
### Evidence
- [M] Table 2a row 3: aAah alone holds; the fall is the same on three board sizes, at 4 and at 5+, for the queen, and in the games to 5 Oct. Table 2d: it costs aAah no dragons at any checkpoint.

- [C] Section 3: the stride already runs for any dragon of 5+ that does not split. [M] 1421767 and 1430530.

- Any turn changes in a game that never has N dragons: the switch reached code it should not.
