# F24: holds in part

Wording of the 06:00 queue (reach on our queen and paid sprints, by rating). The 05:20 verdict on the earlier
wording of this id is kept as F24-0520.md.

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count reproduces. On v31 both halves hold. Against teams rated 2100 or more the
share of our queen's turns with an enemy head within reach is not higher (0.010 against 0.016 at 1700-1899; with
battles resampled the difference runs from -0.015 to +0.004). And the paid share of their rams on her is much
higher: 20 of 26 against 31 of 72 and 33 of 64 (Fisher p 0.005 and 0.034), spread over all 8 opponents (one left
out: 0.73 to 0.82) and not a matter of longer rammers (mean rammer length 3.2, 3.0, 3.3 across the bands; among
rammers of length 3 or 4, 16 of 16 paid at 2100+ against 53 of 70 below, p 0.034). On main the second half does
not show: with the games after battle 1270027 counted the 2100+ cell is 4 of 10 (the queue has 3 of 6), below the
other two bands (14 of 28, 32 of 57), over three opponents. That is too small to contradict v31 (Wilson 0.17 to
0.69), but it is not support, and main is the code that is playing. One thing the first half lets through: reach
is counted at the start of her turn, and by that count the rammer itself was within reach of her at her last turn
start in only 4 of the 26 rams at 2100+ (24 of 72 and 20 of 64 lower down). She was out of its reach, stepped, and
was then inside it in 24 of 26. So "not within reach more often" is true and does not mean she is in no more
danger: rams by r200 per exposed turn are 0.13, 0.19 and 0.51 across the bands on v31.

## Checks

- **Reach, against chance.** v31, 2100+ minus 1700-1899, battles resampled inside each band: -0.015 to +0.004
  (games as units -0.014 to +0.001); minus 1900-2099: -0.022 to +0.002. By layout with 2 or more games in each
  band: higher at 2100+ on 4 layouts, not higher on 10. By opponent at 2100+: 0 of 420, 0 of 425, 0 of 233, 2 of
  650, 6 of 774, 8 of 466, 13 of 795, 16 of 652 exposed turns. Main: -0.006 to +0.010 against 1700-1899; with 17
  games that interval reaches nearly double the lower band's 0.012, so main neither shows more reach nor rules it
  out.
- **What the reach count misses.** The rammer within its own full reach of her head at her last turn start: v31
  24 of 72, 20 of 64, 4 of 26; main 10 of 28, 25 of 57, 4 of 10. Within reach of her head at the ram: v31 71 of 72,
  59 of 64, 24 of 26. Before her last step the rammer was 3 or more path steps away in 20 of 26 rams at 2100+
  against 58 of 144 below (p 0.001). Rams by r200 per exposed turn: v31 0.13, 0.19, 0.51; main 0.21, 0.15, 0.29.
  So against 2100+ teams on v31 the rams come from heads that were not yet counted as within reach.
- **Paid share at 2100+ on v31, by opponent (asked).** Cache me outside 4 of 4, fandagong 3 of 3, Team SKKU 3 of

- **Are paid sprints simply rams by longer dragons (asked).** Length is needed, and it is not the whole story. A
  rammer of length 2 hardly ever pays (v31: 0 of 6, 1 of 29, 1 of 27, 0 of 6 from the lowest band up); length 3
  or 4 pays in 25 of 36, 27 of 32 and 16 of 16; length 5 to 8 in 4 of 5, 5 of 5, 4 of 4. Mean rammer length is
  flat across the bands (3.21, 3.02, 3.31) and paid rammers are longer than unpaid ones in general (3.78 against
  2.45). What differs at 2100+ is the mix and the use: length-2 rammers are 29 of 72 and 27 of 64 lower down and
  6 of 26 at 2100+ (length 3 or more: 20 of 26 against 82 of 144, p 0.08), and inside length 3 or 4 every ram is
  paid (16 of 16 against 53 of 70, p 0.034). Paid counted the direct way (steps in the ramming turn above the free
  steps of the rammer's length at its own turn start) gives the same counts: 31 of 73, 33 of 64, 20 of 26.
