# F25: holds in part

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count in the queue reproduces exactly. The half about the large layouts holds:
with our total at 0.95 of theirs or more at raw round 100, our share is 10 points lower by r300 in 17 of 27 main
games and 35 of 45 v31 games, and 10 points higher by r200 in 2 of 27 and 0 of 45. It is not chance (Fisher against
the other layouts, fall by r300: p 0.0005 on main, 8e-8 on v31). It does not need the r100 condition (all games
that reached r300: 38 of 65 and 58 of 94 against 26 of 121 and 46 of 179). It survives leaving out any layout
name, any opponent and three games, it holds inside bands of our r100 share, and it is not our feeding: in the 333
qualifying games no dragon of ours dies with no action before raw round 201. What does not hold as worded is the
other half, "on other layouts this is rare". There it is about one game in four (12 of 55, 28 of 95), and the other
layouts are not alike: Portals, Trauma and Tower Defense fall by r300 in 21 of 37 qualifying games (both codes),
the same as Around UNSW and Schooltime (20 of 36, Fisher p 1.0), while Maze, weakhold, Autarky, Default and
Slithery Fight fall in 14 of 101. Inside the large group the strong part is Australia and Islands (32 of 36). So
the line the data draws is not at 2000 open tiles: Slithery Fight (1699 tiles, and the largest totals at r100 of
any layout) is among the lowest at 5 of 28, and with four large layout names size cannot be told apart from those
four layouts. Two smaller limits: all 27 main games are from one evening, and on main the fall by r200 is thin
(8 of 27; 5 of 24 without three games, which a Fisher test no longer separates from 8 of 73).
