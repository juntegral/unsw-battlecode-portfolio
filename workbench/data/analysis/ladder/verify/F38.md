# F38: what the queen had beside her at the mouth of a long stay on narrow tiles (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 12:12 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count in the queue recounts exactly from the raw replays. The part that holds: on
main our queen had a free wider neighbour at the mouth of 45 of her 122 long stays, under half in both halves and
both rating bands. The parts that do not:

1. **v31.** 33 of 75 is 0.44 and the interval runs to 0.55. "Well under half" is not shown on v31; without Maze it
   is 19 of 34, without the three games with the most such stays 31 of 61.
2. **Most of these mouths are not entrances.** The definition ends a stay at every tile with three open sides, so a
   walk through a corridor system is cut into several stays at its junctions. 68 of the 122 long stays on main begin
   after a single turn start on a wider tile since her previous stay (v31 30 of 75), and a junction inside a maze
   has no wider neighbour by its geometry (18 of those 68 have another way, 7 of the 30). Where she comes in from
   open water (four or more turn starts on wider tiles before the stay) she had a free wider neighbour in 19 of 27
   on main and 22 of 28 on v31, three in four. So the count does not show that she is forced into corridors; at
   real entrances she mostly had another way and took the narrow tile.
3. **It is three layouts.** Main: Maze, weakhold and Tower Defense 36 of 112, every other layout together 9 of 10.
   v31: 20 of 57 against 13 of 18.
4. **"About a quarter" is the rate of any moment.** 32 of 122 (0.26) and 22 of 75 (0.29) are exact. An enemy head
   is within 3 columns and rows at 0.257 of all her turn starts on a wider tile in the same rounds on main, 0.265
   on v31. The mouth of a long stay is no different from any other turn. The total also hides 1 of 16 in half 1
   against 31 of 106 in half 2, and 9 of 64 against 21 of 56 by rating band.

None of this is particular to our queen: theirs has 10 of 28 on main and 10 of 37 on v31.
