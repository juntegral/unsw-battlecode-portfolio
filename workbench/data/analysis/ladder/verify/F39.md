# F39: death rate of our lone queen by how many short dragons an opponent brings (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 11:17 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.** The counts are exact and the sentence about game results holds. The 1.9 times is a
correct description of these 25 opponent-and-code pairs. It is not shown to be a property of the opponents: the
kind follows the layouts a pair's few lone spells fell on, it is measured in the same games as the deaths, and it
does not carry over to the other games of the same pair.

1. **Recount.** A 30 deaths in 4,843 lone turns (6.19 per 1,000), B 23 in 7,033 (3.27), between 34 in 7,150; main
   4.5 against 2.8, v31 8.7 against 4.9; wins 67 of 110, 70 of 115, 54 of 115. All as the analyst has them.
2. **Chance.** With the pair as the unit the pooled ratio is beyond chance at about one in 50 (kind labels shuffled
   among the 25 pairs: 199 of 10,000 as large; inside each code 384). Neither code is beyond chance alone (main 1.63,
   0.77 to 3.42; v31 1.76, 0.76 to 4.08). Without the three pairs that carry it most: 1.32.
3. **The kind is mostly the layout.** B pairs have 1 to 5 games with lone turns (median 2), A pairs 3 to 12. Bring
   by layout runs from 0.09 (Trauma) and 0.61 (Tower Defense, weakhold) to 6.1 (Islands) and 6.7 (Trophy). What a
   pair's layouts alone give ranks the pairs almost as its own bring does (rank correlation 0.82). 11 of the 17 B
   pairs have most of their fresh turns on Trauma, Tower Defense, weakhold or Maze; no A pair has. The one opponent
   that is A on one code and B on the other (doofenshmirtz: 4.68 on v31, 0.95 on main) is exactly its layouts (4.35
   and 0.86). With the layout held equal A over B is 1.40; with layout and code held, 1.19.
4. **The kind is measured in the games the deaths are in.** Game by game (25 or more fresh turns): bring 3.0 or
   more, she dies in 39 of 39 games; 1.5 to 3.0, 21 of 32; under 1.5, 33 of 67. A high bring is what a hunt that
   ends in her death looks like, so a pair whose lone turns are mostly such games is kind A and has a high rate by
   construction. Kind taken from one random half of a pair's games and deaths from the other half (1,000 splits): A
   5.57, B 4.84 per 1,000; median ratio 1.17, 5th to 95th percentile 0.73 to 1.75; 1.9 or more in 26 splits.
5. **What does stand.** The excess is deaths at the hands of the other side (24 in 4,839 against 15 in 7,031: 4.96
   against 2.13 per 1,000); deaths by wall, own body or no action are level (6 and 8). It is not only the final
   approach: without the last 20 rounds before her death the A pairs still bring 4.60 (4.84 with them). And we win
   more against A (0.61) than against B (0.47), on both codes.

So: in a game where three or more short dragons stand within 12 steps of her at her fresh turns she dies, in 39 of
39 such games. That is a fact about a game. "Kind of opponent" is not established; with this many lone games a pair it cannot be.
