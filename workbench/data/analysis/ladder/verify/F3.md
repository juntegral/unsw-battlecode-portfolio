# F3: our queen dies by wall or own body in about twice as many games as theirs

**Verdict: holds in part.**

Every count comes out the same from the raw replays. Over all 168 games it is 39 against 20 (1.95 times), and that
is more than chance: 31 games where only our queen died this way against 12 where only theirs did (sign test
p = 0.005), and it is not carried by one layout or one opponent. What does not hold as stated:

1. **The factor of two is not stable across the slices.** It is 3.25 (1700-1899), 1.6 (1900-2099), 1.45 (first
   half) and 2.56 (second half). The direction is the same in all four, but in 1900-2099 and in the first half the
   gap is within chance (sign test p = 0.12 and 0.33; battle-resampled interval of the ratio includes 1).
2. **Most of the excess sits in one hour.** In the battles that finished 00:00-00:59 KST on 10-07 it is 16 against
   3 in 61 games; in the rest of the evening 23 against 17 in 107 games (1.35 times). 13 of the 19 excess games are
   in that hour.
3. **It should not be read as "our queen steers into walls".** In all 39 of our games the queen had no safe step
   when she died: every tile next to her head was behind kelp or held a dragon body. The same is true for 18 of
   their 20. A boxed-in queen has to hit something; wall or own body is just what she picked. Counting boxed-in
   queens whatever they hit, it is 49 of ours against 32 of theirs (1.5 times). When boxed in, ours hit a wall or
   herself in 39 of 49 (another dragon's body 7, another dragon's head 3); theirs in 18 of 32 (another dragon's body 4, another dragon's head 8,
   no action 2). So the factor of two is two smaller things multiplied: our queen gets trapped about 1.5 times as
   often, and once trapped theirs more often rams another dragon's head (which also kills that dragon) where ours takes the
   wall.

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own state rebuild (see F7.md); the queen is the lowest starting id of each side
  (0 or 1 in every game); queen death cause, round and length agree with rows.jsonl in all 195 main games.
- **Exclusions.** maps.json flags exactly Schooltime #1, Schooltime #2 and Slithery Fight #1; my layout keys match
  its keys. The 27 excluded games have 2 of ours and 3 of theirs; over all 195 games it is 41 against 23.
- **One layout?** No. Leaving one out gives 1.84 (without Portals) to 2.18 (without Around UNSW). Without the three
  most supporting layouts (Maze 9 v 4, Portals 4 v 1, Devil 6 v 3) it is 20 against 12 (1.67). 2-start layouts 21
  against 11, the other kept layouts 18 against 9. It never happens to our queen on Queen Of Spades (17 games),
  Trauma (18), Default (15), Autarky (8).
- **One opponent?** No. At most 5 from one opponent (9 games). Leaving one out gives 1.75 to 2.44.
- **One evening?** All main games are from one evening, so not testable; by hour see point 2. By hour (games, ours,
  theirs): 10-06 07h 5, 3, 0; 19h 5, 1, 0; 20h 13, 1, 1; 21h 29, 8, 7; 22h 12, 1, 0; 23h 26, 4, 5; 10-07 00h 61,
  16, 3; 01h 13, 3, 3; 02h 4, 2, 1. By bot id: 18504 30 v 15 in 120 games, 18570 6 v 5 in 43, 18066 3 v 0 in 5.
- **When.** Ours: median round 120; 16 by r100, 20 in r101-300, 3 later. Theirs: median round 168.5; 8 by r100, 4 in
  r101-300, 8 later. Up to r300 it is 36 against 12; after r300 it is 3 against 8. Median queen length at death 3
  (ours) and 2 (theirs).
- **Is it just the lost games?** No. Ours happened in 22 games we won and 17 we lost (96 of the 168 were won).
- **Competing causes.** Queen fate in the 168 games, ours / theirs: alive at the end 38 / 44; wall 26 / 15; own body
  13 / 5; other dragon's body 8 / 4; head-on 83 / 97; no action 0 / 3. Their queens are not dying earlier of
  something else in a way that would hide wall deaths: total deaths are 130 and 124. In head-on deaths the queen was
  the stationary one in 78 of our 83 and 85 of their 97.
- **Boxed in (point 3).** Definition: on her own turn, at the first step of her action, each of the four tiles next
  to her head is behind kelp or holds a segment of a living dragon (her own included, tail too) or of a dragon that
  died earlier in that turn. Boxed-in queen deaths by round, ours / theirs: r0-100 20 / 15; r101-300 23 / 11; later
  6 / 6. In games we won / lost: ours 29 / 20, theirs 23 / 9. The two of their 20 that had a free step are own-body
  deaths after r300.
