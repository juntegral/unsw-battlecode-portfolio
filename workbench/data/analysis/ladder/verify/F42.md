# F42: the sighting rule in the turns of their lone queen (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 18:20 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count in the queue recounts exactly from the raw replays. What holds: on main we
hold a sighting 20 rounds old or less in 0.505 of her lone turns and on v31 in 0.341 (game labels shuffled between
the codes: a gap as large in 445 of 20,000); in those turns a head of ours is within 6 path steps in 0.190 and
0.120; their heads against our lone queen have 0.343 and 0.349, and that gap survives when the layouts are matched.
What is weaker than the wording:

   1200 cells or fewer, in the turns in which her head is in the box of a head of ours right now (age 0), no head of
   ours is within 12 path steps in 1,246 of 1,746 turns (both codes; main 684 of 1,005, v31 562 of 741). On the big

   and "near when fresh" on small layouts measures the walls of Maze and Tower Defense (29 of the 43 small games),
   not a failure to walk six steps.
2. **The same happens to them.** In the mirror games on Trauma their heads see our queen in 292 turns and are within
   12 path steps in 9; on Maze 1 of 26. On the small layouts without Maze, Tower Defense, Autarky, Trauma and Stripes
   their heads are near in 0.51 of the fresh turns (7 games) and ours in 0.20 (7 games).
3. **Main against v31 differ in the sighting, not in the nearness.** Fresh 0.505 against 0.341 is a real gap (445
   of 20,000). Near when fresh 0.190 against 0.120 is not (4,599 of 20,000 shuffles), and three games hold much of
   each: without the three with most fresh-and-near turns 0.144 on main and 0.085 on v31. 14 of the 40 games on main
   and 13 of 30 on v31 have no fresh-and-near turn at all.
4. **A sighting older than five rounds is worth little.** Main without Portals, near by age of the sighting: age 0
   672 of 1,538, 1 to 5 rounds 347 of 1,898, 6 to 10 58 of 1,145, 11 to 20 62 of 1,419. Of the "0-20" turns 43 in
   100 are 6 rounds old or more, and a head is near in 0.05 of those. The 0.19 is mostly 0.44 at age 0 and 0.18 at
   ages 1 to 5.
5. **The mirror is 24 games, none against 2100+.** 14 of them are against teams under 1900. Main alone: fresh 909
   and near when fresh 1,922 of 20,000 shuffles; v31 1,097 and 950; both codes 66 and 208.

**Plainer explanation: more heads see more.** No. We have about 32 heads alive in these turns and they about 23 in
the mirror, and they see more with fewer. If the heads stood on random tiles her head would be in some box in about
half the turns (0.52 on main, 0.48 in the mirror); it is in 0.13 and 0.24, so on both sides the heads are away
from the surviving queen far more than position alone gives.

**What the measure cannot tell.** A turn is fresh or near when two heads are close; either may have moved. It reads
the same if their queen keeps away from our heads and ours does not keep away from theirs.
