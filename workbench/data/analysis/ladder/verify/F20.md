# F20: holds in part

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every number reproduces exactly. What holds: the gap is there against every rating
band in both codes; it is already there at rounds 50 and 75 (main 0.319 against 0.402, v31 0.338 against 0.437);
and per game a bigger gap goes with a smaller share of the pearls on Around UNSW (-0.68) and Slithery Fight
(-0.62), also when the busy tiles are taken from the other games on the layout (-0.67 and -0.65). What does not
hold: "about the same size" on main. The gap is 0.164 against 1700-1899 teams, 0.074 against 1900-2099 and 0.138
against 2100+; the first two differ by more than chance (band labels shuffled between games inside each layout
reach the difference in 3 of 4000) and in the same order inside Around UNSW (0.146, 0.095), Slithery Fight (0.211,
0.051) and Schooltime #2 (0.160, 0.102). On v31 the bands agree (0.102, 0.116, 0.112, 0.117). Two limits on the
cost part: the correlation comes from where their heads stand, not ours (their share alone -0.68 and -0.56, ours
alone +0.21 and +0.27, the latter within chance), and it does not show in results (bigger-gap half won 18 of 36,
smaller-gap half 20 of 36).

## Checks

- **Same size across bands?** Gap with a game bootstrap interval. Main: 1700-1899 0.164 (0.118 to 0.213, 29
  games), 1900-2099 0.074 (0.046 to 0.102, 38 games), 2100+ 0.138 (0.107 to 0.159, 6 games). v31: under 1700 0.102
  (-0.013 to 0.171, 4 games), 1700-1899 0.116 (0.076 to 0.160), 1900-2099 0.112 (0.080 to 0.141), 2100+ 0.117
  (0.028 to 0.238, 18 games). Difference between the two middle bands, labels shuffled inside each layout: main
  +0.090, reached by 3 of 4000; v31 +0.004, reached by 3568 of 4000. Inside a layout on main: Around UNSW 0.146
  (25 snapshots), 0.095 (60), 0.138 (13); Slithery Fight 0.211 (18), 0.051 (46), 0.161 (5); Schooltime #2 0.160
  (13), 0.102 (6). So on main the gap is present everywhere but about half as large against 1900-2099 teams; there
  is no trend with rating in either code.
- **Bands with the busy tiles taken from the other games** (8 or more births in at least half of them). Main 0.112,
  0.055, 0.096; v31 0.065, 0.082, 0.083, 0.097. The same picture, about a quarter smaller.
- **Rounds 50 and 75.** Gap with a game bootstrap interval: main 0.083 (0.032 to 0.131), v31 0.099 (0.056 to
  0.143). By game, theirs higher against ours higher: main 18 against 9, v31 29 against 13. Round 50 alone 0.085
  and 0.058; round 75 alone 0.081 and 0.137. With other-game busy tiles 0.074 (0.038 to 0.111) and 0.089 (0.055
  to 0.121). Only 36 of 146 early snapshots on main and 57 of 238 on v31 have level head counts; over all early
  snapshots it is 0.322 against 0.402 and 0.319 against 0.404. As in the later rounds it sits on Around UNSW and
  Slithery Fight; nothing on Australia (-0.025, +0.004), Islands (-0.007, +0.042) or Schooltime #2 (-0.020,
  -0.030).
- **Cost: chance.** Rank shuffles reaching the correlation, of 4000: Around UNSW together 0, main 379, v31 0;
  Slithery Fight together 1, main 428, v31 40. Main alone is within chance on both layouts; v31 carries it.
- **Cost: is it the definition?** With the busy tiles from the other games: Around UNSW -0.67 (main -0.36, v31
  -0.82), Slithery Fight -0.65 (-0.65, -0.61). Not an artefact of naming the busy tiles from the same game.
- **Cost: whose heads?** Their share of heads near busy tiles alone against our pearl share: -0.68 and -0.56 (0
  and 1 of 4000). Our share alone: +0.21 and +0.27 (818 and 502 of 4000). The number of busy tiles in the game
  against our pearl share: -0.22 and -0.53. So the relation is "the more their heads sit on the supply, the less
  we eat"; our own placement explains little of it at this size.
- **Cost: results.** Mean pearl share, smaller-gap half against bigger-gap half: 0.379 against 0.313 on Around
  UNSW, 0.431 against 0.354 on Slithery Fight. Wins 13 of 20 against 10 of 20 and 7 of 16 against 8 of 16. Mean
  gap in games won and lost: 0.133 and 0.140; 0.119 and 0.123.
- **Cost: three games, one opponent, one evening.** Without the three games that support it most: -0.61 and
  -0.50. Leave one opponent out: -0.73 to -0.65 and -0.66 to -0.58. Leave one half-day out: -0.78 to -0.53 and
  -0.65 to -0.53.
- **Not checked.** Direction: gap and pearl share are measured over the same rounds, so this does not say that
  the gap comes first; an early gap against a later pearl share was not run.
