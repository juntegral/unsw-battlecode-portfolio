# F13: holds in part

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count in the claim reproduces exactly, in every slice. What does not hold is
the weight the sentence puts on them. (1) "We win every game in which she survives" is the queen rule, not a
result: with their queen dead and ours alive the game cannot be lost (24 of 24 and 47 of 47; in the mirror we win
0 of 31 and 0 of 61). (2) "Our queen still dies later in about two games out of three" is not about our queen:
when ours dies first, theirs dies later at the same rate (main 60 of 91 = 0.659 against 47 of 71 = 0.662, Fisher
p = 1.0; v31 121 of 182 = 0.665 against 112 of 159 = 0.704, p = 0.48). It is how often a queen dies in these
games (ours in 141 of 180 main games, theirs in 134). (3) "Only about half" is what is left after the sure wins
are taken out, and it is a 1900-2099 number (17 of 33); at 1700-1899 it is 9 of 12. Over all theirs-first games
we win 50 of 71 (0.70) on main and 92 of 159 (0.58) on v31, against 41 of 91 (0.45) and 73 of 182 (0.40) when
ours dies first. The part that stands as a finding: in 47 of 180 main games we had the queen lead and then lost
her, 21 of those 47 were lost, and 30 of the 47 deaths were an opponent dragon moving into her head.

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own state rebuild from the raw events (only the byte decoder is shared). Final
  state equals the replay's result block in 620 of 620 main and v31 games, no anomalies. Compared with rows.jsonl
  only afterwards: code, layout, win, last round, both queen death rounds agree in 620 of 620.
- **The rule does the first part.** Of the 24 main survivor games 7 ended at r499 (queen rule) and 17 by
  elimination; v31 8 and 39. A side with a living queen cannot be the eliminated side, and at r499 a living queen
  beats a dead one. This part cannot come out any other way.
- **Symmetry.** See the verdict. Queen ever dies: main ours 0.78, theirs 0.74; v31 0.83 and 0.79.
- **Exposure.** Deaths per queen-round, before / after the other queen is dead, main, our queen: r0-99 0.0037 /
  0.0074, r100-199 0.0049 / 0.0048, r200-299 0.0031 / 0.0045, r300-399 0.0018 / 0.0064, r400-499 0.0012 /
  0.0044. Their queen in the mirror: 0.0037 / 0.0088, 0.0028 / 0.0052, 0.0013 / 0.0049, 0.0005 / 0.0010, 0.0018 /
  0.0036. v31 the same shape (ours r0-99 0.0041 / 0.0083, r300-399 0.0023 / 0.0067; theirs 0.0043 / 0.0087,
  0.0009 / 0.0025). So a queen is roughly twice as likely to die per round once the other queen is gone, on both
  sides. Either the side that lost its queen goes hunting, or some games are simply bloodier for both; these
  counts cannot tell the two apart. What they do rule out is that our queen gets safer once we lead.
- **Timing.** Main, dies-later games: their queen dies at median r79, ours at median r161, gap median 60 rounds;
  10 of 47 within 25 rounds, 19 of 47 a hundred or more rounds later. v31: r63.5, r151, gap 54.5; 29 and 39 of
  112.
- **One opponent, map or evening.** Main: 22 opponents in the 71 games, the largest two have 9 each. Leave one
  opponent out: dies-later share 0.652 to 0.697, win share among them 0.512 to 0.595. Leave one layout out: 0.625
  to 0.705 and 0.475 to 0.619. Leave one evening out: 0.634 to 0.676. By layout it is uneven (Queen Of Spades 4

  Islands 0 of 5). v31 leave one opponent out 0.687 to 0.748; leave one layout out 0.682 to 0.750.
- **Rating.** Dies-later share rises with opponent rating and the wins fall: main 12 of 22 (wins 9 of 12) at
  1700-1899, 33 of 47 (17 of 33) at 1900-2099, 2 of 2 (0) at 2100+. v31: 54 of 90 (26 of 54), 36 of 46 (12 of

- **Chance.** Wilson 95 percent: 47 of 71 is 0.55 to 0.76; 26 of 47 is 0.41 to 0.69; v31 112 of 159 is 0.63 to
  0.77 and 45 of 112 is 0.32 to 0.49. Without three supporting games: 44 of 68 = 0.647; 26 of 44 = 0.59.

  That is not a paradox: the theirs-first games we were winning easily left this group as survivor wins, and the

- **Cause.** Rammed by an enemy is the main cause for both queens: ours 30 of 47 here and 83 of 141 over all
  main deaths (0.59); theirs 39 of 60 in the mirror and 89 of 134 over all (0.66). Rammer length median 3, our
