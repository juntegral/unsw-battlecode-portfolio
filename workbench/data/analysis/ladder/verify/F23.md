# F23: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every number in the queue reproduces exactly, in both slices and in the two contrasts. In all
36 games our queen takes her first portal step in raw round 4 and starts round 5 in a 4-tile room; she is dead in
every game, in a room of 6 tiles or fewer in 9 of 9 on main and 26 of 27 on v31. Their queen takes a portal step in
2 of 9 and 12 of 27, dies in a small room in 1 and 10, and is alive at the end in 4 and 6. Nothing carries it: 9
opponents in the 9 main games, 20 in the 27 v31 games, three half-days, both sides of the board. Two things the
sentence can hide. First, the room she enters at round 5 is usually not where she dies: she dies on that first
visit in 2 of 9 main games and 12 of 27 v31 games; in the rest she leaves, keeps going back into small rooms, and
dies on a later visit (7 and 14 games), mostly in a 1-tile room. Second, the rooms are not safe for their queens
either: a 1-tile room killed every queen that started a turn in one (ours 16 of 16, theirs 9 of 9). The difference
is that ours goes in from round 4 in every game, and that the 4-tile rooms kill ours in 19 of 57 visits and theirs
in 2 of 27.

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own state rebuild and own board graph; a room is the set of tiles joined over edges
  that are neither kelp nor portal, wrap included. A portal step is read from the queen's own action direction and
  the edge kind, and agrees with the geometry in every step. 685 of 685 games read, final state equal to the
  replay's result block in all 645 main and v31 games, no anomalies. Compared with rows.jsonl only afterwards.
- **The layout.** 32 by 16, 512 open tiles, 20 portal pairs. Two rooms of 208 tiles, twelve of 4, sixteen of 2 and
  sixteen of 1. Both queens start 3 long in a 208-tile room.
- **The same opening every game.** First portal step in raw round 4 and a 4-tile room at the start of round 5 in
  36 of 36. She grows there (3 long at the start, 4 or 5 long at the ten round-9 deaths on v31), so "pearl room"
  fits; I did not count pearls per room.
- **First visit against later visits.** Died on the first visit: main 2 of 9 (round 10 both), v31 12 of 27 (round
  9 ten times, round 10 twice; all twelve by running into the body of another dragon of ours). Fisher main against
  v31 p 0.43, so no sign that main is better at it. Died on a later visit: 7 and 14. Visits to 4-tile rooms and
  deaths in them: ours 20 and 4 on main, 37 and 15 on v31; theirs 1 and 0, 26 and 2. Visits to 1-tile rooms and
  deaths: ours 5 and 5, 11 and 11; theirs 1 and 1, 8 and 8.
- **Given that she went in.** Queens that ever started a turn in a small room and died in one: ours 35 of 36,
  theirs 11 of 14 (Fisher p 0.06). So most of the gap between the sides is going in at all (36 of 36 against 14
  of 36), not what happens once inside.
- **Chance.** Died in a small room, ours against theirs: Fisher p 0.0004 on main, below 0.00001 on v31. Portal
  step: p 0.002 and below 0.00001. Wilson 95 percent: 9 of 9 is 0.70 to 1.00, 26 of 27 is 0.82 to 0.99. Without
  three supporting games: 6 of 6 and 23 of 24.
- **One opponent, map, evening.** One layout by definition. Main: 9 opponents, one game each; half-days 1, 4, 4;
  bands 4, 3, 2; our side A 6 times, B 3. v31: 20 opponents, at most 3 games each; half-days 17 and 10; side A 19,
  B 8.
- **What it costs.** All 36 games ran the full 500 rounds. Their queen alive at the end: we won 0 of 10 (ours is
  never alive, so the queen rule decides). Their queen dead: we won 17 of 26. Our queen dead by round 10: won 8 of
  14; later: 9 of 22. An early death is not what loses; never having a queen at the end is.
- **Other layouts.** Rooms of 6 or fewer that a queen ever starts a turn in exist on Maze (ours in 5 of 18 main
  and 6 of 24 v31 games, died there in 1 and 3), Stripes (8 of 10 and 22 of 26, died 4 and 5) and the Schooltime
  vault (every game, none of ours died). Portals is the only layout where she always goes in and nearly always
  dies inside.
- **Not checked.** Whether the dragon she runs into at round 9 is her own child; whether the pearls she goes for
  could be had without her.
