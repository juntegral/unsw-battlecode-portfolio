# F39, new part: short dragons within 12 steps of a lone queen, and how fresh her last sighting is (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 10:21 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.** The counts are exact, the approximation in "seen" is harmless and the direction holds on
both codes. The sizes ("about a third", "half as many") are pooled per-turn figures. On main they belong to full games
against opponents rated 1900 and above, and they do not show in how often the lone queen dies.

Every number in the queue recounts to the last turn from the raw replays (785 games, own rebuild). Testing "seen" at
the turn starts of the other side's dragons, which is when a bot gets its window, moves the fresh share by 0.015 at
most. With the game as the unit the direction holds on both codes. Three limits on the sizes:

1. "About a third" depends on where the lone turns fall. Their lone queen's turns pile up on layouts where nobody
   is near a lone queen (Around UNSW, Maze, Tower Defense, weakhold) and ours on the small boards where everybody
   is. With the layout mix held equal, short heads within 12 a turn are 0.57 to 0.87 of theirs on main and 0.47 to
   0.66 on v31; per game the mean share is 0.69 and 0.54 of theirs.
2. On main the gap is a full-game one. In games that ended by elimination the share is 0.119 against 0.116. At
   fresh turns "half as many" is 0.95 against opponents rated 1700-1899 (within chance) and 0.37 against 1900 and
   above: what moves is how many of THEIR short dragons stand near our queen (1.44 against 2.78 a fresh turn),
   ours near theirs stay at 1.0 to 1.4. Without three games of ours (two Islands, one Default) the main figure is
   0.64. On v31 it is 0.31 to 0.47 in every slice.
3. On main it does not show in the result of the hunt. Their lone queen dies in 88 of 133 lone spells, 4.7 per
   1,000 lone turns; ours in 71 of 113, 5.0 per 1,000. On v31 it does show: 4.3 against 7.9 per 1,000.

"About 6 turns in 10" holds for main as counted (0.596; battle bootstrap 0.51 to 0.68). It is 0.50 on the three big
layouts and 0.49 on v31, and it counts any tile of her body, not her head.

In this file "ours" means our short dragons near THEIR lone queen and "theirs" means their short dragons near OUR
lone queen, in that order, as in the queue.
