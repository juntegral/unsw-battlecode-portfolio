**Verdict: holds in part.**

The core holds firmly. The count is right to the last game (main ours 21 of 145, theirs 1 of 142; v31 30 of 175
against 11 of 191), it is far beyond chance on both codes, our share is the higher one on 9 of 14 layouts on main
and 7 of 13 on v31 with none and one the other way, no opponent or evening carries it, and it survives taking

theirs in that position often ends the turn in a way the two engine reasons do not catch (on main 4 of their 6
ran head-on into one of our dragons). Counting every boxed-in queen whatever the engine wrote down gives main
24 of 145 against 6 of 142 (3.9 times) and v31 38 of 175 against 19 of 191 (2.2 times). On v31 it is two to three
times by any count. Second, Maze carries 37 percent (19 of 51), not nearly half; Maze, Portals and weakhold

0 of 103, p 0.007) and is not there on v31 (8 of 131 against 4 of 129, p 0.38; every boxed-in death 11 of 131
against 10 of 129). Third, the other windows are level only in the sense that none differs beyond chance: per
queen alive at the start of the window, r201-300 on main is 9 of 64 against 5 of 94 (2.7 times, p 0.085) and
r301-499 leans the other way (v31 1 of 44 against 10 of 84).
