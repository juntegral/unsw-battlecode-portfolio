# F10: holds in part

**Verdict: holds in part.** The first half holds firmly: on Tower Defense, Stripes, Trauma and Queen Of Spades
together our share of the pearls eaten by raw round 50 is 0.331 on main and 0.296 on v31, against 0.509 and 0.507
on all other layouts, and every count reproduces exactly. The second half, that on main the gap is absent against
opponents rated 1700-1899, is true as counted (108 to 112 in 19 games) but only for part of what it names. Inside
that slice we are ahead on Queen Of Spades (4 games of 4) and Tower Defense (3 of 6, behind in 1) and still far
behind on Trauma (29 to 87, behind in 6 games of 7). The pooled 0.491 is three Queen Of Spades games set against
four Trauma games: without our three best games it is 0.329. Counted by game it is steadier (ahead in 9, level in
2, behind in 7; median game 0.55, against 0.26 at 1900-2099 on main and 0.34 on v31 in the same band). That
difference is beyond chance against main 1900-2099 (p = 0.02) but not against v31 in the same band (p = 0.10
to 0.13). So it should read: on main against 1700-1899 the deficit is gone on Queen Of Spades and Tower Defense, in
10 games, and unchanged on Trauma; that main differs from v31 there is not shown. Two smaller points: scarcity
alone is not the cause (the other layouts the rule classes as scarce are not low), and Trauma has the lowest
share on main although both sides split there at once, so late splitting cannot be the whole reason.

## Checks

- **Chance for the first half.** Bootstrap over games, 95 percent: four layouts 0.249 to 0.424 on main, 0.245 to
  0.352 on v31; all other layouts 0.486 to 0.534 and 0.491 to 0.526. Shuffling the scarce label over games: 0 of
  2000. By layout the intervals are wide because so little is eaten: Tower Defense 0.30 to 0.48 (81 pearls in 15
  games, the median game has 5), Stripes 0.17 to 0.47, Trauma 0.13 to 0.27, Queen Of Spades 0.27 to 0.63 on
  main. Queen Of Spades on main is not separable from one half on its own.
- **Not every game.** Our share is under one half in 37 of the 59 main games on the four layouts and in 63 of
  91 on v31. The pooled third is a majority of games plus a few heavy ones, not a uniform deficit.
- **Is it scarcity?** The other layouts the rule calls scarce are not low: Schooltime #1 0.537 main and 0.438
  v31, Schooltime #2 0.449 v31, Default 0.557 v31. With the cutoff at 20 or 25 the scarce class on main is the
  three layouts Tower Defense, Stripes, Trauma plus Schooltime #1 and gives 0.304; at 40 it takes in three more
  and gives 0.411. And the two-dragon layouts that are not scarce are level: Maze 0.503 and 0.516, Trophy 0.488
  and 0.533, weakhold 0.596 and 0.545. So the low share belongs to these four layouts; neither "scarce" nor
  "two dragons at the start" alone predicts it. Low without being scarce: Australia 0.291 main, 0.337 v31.
- **Splits.** On Stripes and Queen Of Spades our first split is later in both codes (main 24 against 18 and 31
  against 12; v31 23 against 15 and 26 against 12). On Tower Defense it is later on main (52 against 43) but
  earlier on v31 (38.5 against 42.5) with the same share (0.37, 0.38). On Trauma both sides split at r0 or r1
  and our share is the lowest of the four on main. The split timing fits two of the four.
- **The 1700-1899 statement on main.** 19 games, ours 108, theirs 112, bootstrap 95 percent 0.30 to 0.71. By
  layout (games, pearls, games ahead / behind): Queen Of Spades 4, 62 to 12, 4 / 0; Tower Defense 6, 13 to 12,
