# F20: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every number reproduces exactly, including the per-layout table and the nearest-friend count
(whose definition I had to guess, see below). At snapshots with level head counts, 0.318 of their heads and 0.212
of ours are within 2 tiles of a busy tile on main (0.317 and 0.203 on v31). It holds on Around UNSW and on
Slithery Fight separately, by game (their share higher in 38 games, ours in 6), for every radius and threshold I
tried, and it survives the obvious objection that the definition is circular. That objection is real: a tile is
busy in a game only if somebody keeps eating there, they eat 62 percent of the pearls on busy tiles, and each
side's heads sit next to the tiles it eats from. But when the busy tiles are taken from the other games on the
same layout the gap is still there (0.193 against 0.269 on main), and on Around UNSW it is there for the map's
real fast tiles (0.063 against 0.109). Limits: two layouts carry it (160 of 197 kept snapshots); on Australia
there is no difference (v31: 0.124 against 0.124 over 30 snapshots); about a third of the gap comes from the
definition; and on the real fast tiles our share of the eating is no lower than elsewhere.
