# F7: stall on 2-start layouts, and its cause on Tower Defense

**Verdict: holds in part.**

Every count in the claim and in Part B comes out the same from the raw replays. What holds: on 2-start layouts main
had at most 6 dragons against at least 15 at raw round 100 in 15 of 84 games and lost 13 of them; with this rule it
is 0 of 103 on the other layouts; and on Tower Defense our heads stay about 36 steps from the nearest pearl through
rounds 0 to 50 while the opponent walks to the pearls. What is weaker than the wording:

1. It is not a property of 2-start layouts in general. 8 of the 15 are Tower Defense (8 of its 13 games). Three of
   the seven 2-start layouts (Maze, Stripes, weakhold: 31 games) have none. Without Tower Defense it is 7 of 71.
2. "Does not happen on other layouts" is partly built into the "ours <= 6" threshold. Those layouts start with 3 to
   10 dragons per team, and only 2 of 103 games there have ours <= 6 at r100 at all. With a rule that does not depend
   on the starting count (theirs >= 15 and theirs >= 2.5 x ours) it is 17 of 84 against 3 of 103 at r100, and 18 of 66
   against 9 of 91 at r200. So it is concentrated on 2-start layouts at r100, not absent elsewhere.
3. The 15 games are two different things. In 11 (Tower Defense 8, Queen Of Spades 3) we ate 2 to 6 pearls in 101
   rounds. In 4 (Trophy 2, Trauma 2) we ate 17, 20, 25 and 41 pearls and still had at most 6 dragons: those are lost
   dragons, not unreached food. The median of 4 pearls hides this.
4. All 195 main games are from one evening (battles from 10-06 07:35 to 10-07 02:08 KST, 190 of them after 19:00), so
   nothing here says it lasts across days.

Part B holds, for Tower Defense only: it does not explain the 7 stalls on the other three layouts.

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own state rebuild from the replay events (only the byte decoder is shared):
  195 main games (ids 18504: 140, 18570: 50, 18066: 5), final dragons / longest / total equal to the replay's result
  block in 195 of 195, no event anomalies. My step rule (kelp blocks, portal leads to the tile beyond the partner
  edge, board wraps) predicted all 4,459,994 moves in these games, 38,520 of them through a wrap or portal, with 0
  misses. At the end I compared with rows.jsonl: layout key, main flag, bot id, result, r100 alive / total / pearls,
  pearls r0-100 agree in every game.
- **One opponent?** No. 9 opponents, at most 3 from one (free trip to sydney pls, 3 of its 7 games); 12 of 39
  battles. Leaving any one opponent out leaves 12 to 14 stalls.
- **One map?** Partly, see point 1. Without Tower Defense: 7 of 71 against 0 of 103 (Fisher exact about 0.002, but
  see point 2). Without Queen Of Spades 12 of 67, without Trauma 13 of 66, without Trophy 13 of 79.
- **One evening?** Cannot be tested: every main game is from the same evening. First 97 main games: 6 of 43; last
  98: 9 of 41. By bot id: 18504 13 of 60, 18570 2 of 22, 18066 0 of 2.
- **Three strongest games removed** (Trophy 3 v 51, Trophy 5 v 43, Tower Defense 2 v 25): 12 of 81 against 0 of 103.
- **Chance.** 15 of 84 against 0 of 103: Fisher exact p = 3e-06. Lost 13 of 15 against 25 lost of the 69 other
  2-start games (44 won): p = 0.0004. Games of one battle are not independent, so read these as "far from chance",
  not as exact.
- **Threshold.** Stall on 2-start / on other layouts at r100: ours<=4, theirs>=15: 13 / 0; ours<=6, theirs>=12:
  19 / 1; ours<=6, theirs>=20: 8 / 0; ours<=8, theirs>=15: 18 / 2; ours<=10, theirs>=15: 22 / 3. Ratio rule
  (theirs >= 15 and >= 2.5 x ours): r100 17 of 84 / 3 of 103; r150 21 of 73 / 2 of 98; r200 18 of 66 / 9 of 91.
- **The other direction on other layouts.** The opponent is the one with <= 6 against our >= 15 in 13 of 103 games
  there (4 of 84 on 2-start layouts).
- **Our pearls r0-100 in the 15 stall games:** 5, 4, 3, 4, 2, 2, 4, 6, 25, 17, 5, 41, 2, 3, 20.

Part B:

- At round 0 the two sides are equally far from a pearl (35 and 35 steps, or 33 and 33, or 35 and 33).
- Our per-game median path is 33 to 39 in 12 of the 13 games (9 in one), stall or not. Our pearls r0-100 are 2 to
  6 in all 13 games, stall or not. So on Tower Defense we are stuck every time; "stall" only marks the games where
  the opponent got out. In the 5 other games the opponent was slow too (their pearls 1, 5, 7, 14, 42); game 1254772
  (3 v 13 at r100, they ate 42) is a stall that falls just under the "theirs >= 15" line. We won 4 of the 13.
- **Plainer explanation tested: "their path is short because they have more heads".** No. Their path first drops
  below 20 while they still have their 2 starting heads in 12 of 13 games (in the 13th it never drops). Rounds 0-10
  only: ours 36, theirs 31.5 (stall games) and 32 (other games).
- Their path falls about one step per round, a straight walk (game 1249037 at every fifth round: 35, 31, 26, 21,
  16, 11, 6, 1). Ours moves between 33 and 41 without a trend.
- The same measure on the other 2-start layouts shows no Tower Defense-like gap in stall games: Queen Of Spades
  8 v 2 (other games 5.5 v 4), Trophy 3 v 1.5 (2 v 2). On Trauma our heads are about twice as far from pearls as
  theirs in every game, stall or not (14 v 6 and 14.5 v 6.5).
- What Part B shows is that we do not approach the pearls on Tower Defense. It does not show why (the replay cannot
  say whether the bot fails to plan the 35-step route or decides not to take it).
