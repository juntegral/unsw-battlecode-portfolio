# F39: short enemy heads near the lone queen (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 09:18 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.** The direction holds, firmly on v31; the size "two and a half to four times" does
not, and on main the gap is neither robust nor special to lone turns.

The counts are right to the last turn. The gap is not a matter of how many short dragons are alive: our lone
queen and theirs face the same number of short enemy dragons (27.1 against 27.9 per lone turn in main full
games, 27.4 against 26.4 on v31) and have the same reach (58 against 56 tiles within 6 steps), and with that
number held equal the shares do not move (main 0.259 against 0.102, v31 0.237 against 0.055). What weakens the
claim is the mix of layouts and the unit. Portals #1 holds 9,176 of their 42,167 full-game lone turns and 156
of ours, and there the flag cannot fire at all (two kelp-sealed halves: 0 in 14,372 queen turns of either
side). Without Portals the full-game ratio is 2.1 on main and 3.3 on v31, and over all endings 1.6 and 2.7.
Per game instead of per turn the mean share is 0.253 against 0.188 on main (1.35 times) and 0.319 against 0.131
on v31 (2.4 times). On the small boards (Default, Queen Of Spades, Trophy, Devil, both Prisoners Dilemma) the
share near their lone queen is as high as near ours or higher. On main the 1700-1899 band (1.38 times) is not
beyond chance, and the same gap is already there with both queens alive from r201 (0.060 against 0.017, then
0.113 against 0.023), so on main it does not start when she is alone; on v31 it does (1.0 to 1.4 times with
both alive). The second half, fewer own heads within 5 tiles, holds on v31 and is thin on main.

## Other checks

**Chance, with the game as the unit** (lone turns come in runs of hundreds inside one game). Shuffling the
labels ours and theirs among the games that have lone turns, pooled share of ours minus theirs: main full 2 of
10,000 shuffles at least as large, v31 full 0, main all endings 2, v31 all endings 0, level pooled 0; main full
1700-1899 2,178 of 10,000 (per game mean 0.159 against 0.181); main full 1900-2099 1 of 10,000. Battle
bootstrap of the ratio, 95 percent: main full 1.5 to 4.0, v31 full 2.9 to 6.5, main all endings 1.3 to 2.6, v31
all endings 2.6 to 4.6, level pooled 1.8 to 5.1. The shuffle does not hold the layout equal, so it shares the
Portals tilt. The 2100+ band on main is 2 games of ours.

**Games that ended early go the other way on main.** Lone turns in games that ended by elimination: main 0.265
of 6,095 against 0.388 of 2,807 (0.68 times); v31 0.344 of 5,061 against 0.189 of 5,533 (1.8 times). That is
why main over all endings is 1.85 and not 2.5.

**On main it is not a lone-turn effect.** With both queens alive the same flag is 1.2 and 1.4 times higher for
our queen in the first two hundred rounds, 3.6 times in r201-300 (262 of 4,345 against 72 of 4,343) and 5.0
times from r301 (602 of 5,330 against 121 of 5,332), more than in lone turns. On v31 the both-alive rates are
1.0 to 1.4 times apart in every window and the lone-turn gap is 3.5 to 4.4, so there the gap does belong to
lone turns.
