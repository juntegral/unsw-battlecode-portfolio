   The flag says the longest-dragon rule would have gone our way if their queen had been dead too; nothing more.
   On v31 this is milder (one game with three or fewer dragons left).

body, or the body of another dragon of ours): flagged 10 of 15 on main and 8 of 13 on v31; kept but not flagged 8 of
