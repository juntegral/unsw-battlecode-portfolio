# F13: holds in part

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count reproduces exactly in every slice. The first half stands: when our queen
is rammed after theirs has died she has fewer than two friends within 5 tiles in 27 of 30 rams on main (57 of 75 on
v31), and that is more alone than she is over all her turns in that state (0.58 of them). The second half is true
as counted (17 of 27 on main, 59 of 75 on v31) but it does not show a way out that she missed. In 15 of those 17
main rams (43 of the 59 on v31) she did end her turn beyond the rammer's free steps and was rammed anyway, because
the rammer paid length for extra steps: it made more steps than its free steps in 17 of the 30 rams. Only 2 of 27
(16 of 75 on v31) are rams in which she ended inside the free reach while another step led out of it. Measured
against the distance the rammer really covered, a farther step existed in 11 of 27 on main (0.41, interval 0.25 to
0.59, so not "most") and in 43 of 75 on v31; measured against everything the rammer could have paid for, in 4 of 27
and 25 of 75. None of it is particular to our queen: their lone queen rammed by ours gives the same picture (35 of
42 alone, 25 of 41, 13 of 41).

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own state rebuild and own board graph (kelp closed, portals followed, wrap). Final
  state equals the replay's result block in 635 of 635 main and v31 games, no anomalies, 28089 head-on pairs all in
  the order victim then mover. Compared with rows.jsonl only afterwards: code, win, last round, both queen death
  rounds and final dragon counts agree in every game.
- **What "a step beyond the free steps" counts.** Split of the 17 main rams: in 15 she ended her last turn on a
  tile the rammer could not reach with free steps and was rammed all the same; in 2 she ended inside the free
  reach although another step led out. v31: 43 and 16. Their lone queen: main 15 and 10, v31 26 and 7. The rammer's
  steps in the ram turn (main, 30 rams): 1 step 11 times, 2 steps 8, 3 steps 6, 4 steps 4, 6 steps once; it was
  2 or 3 long in 21 of 30. A rammer dies in the ram anyway, so paying length costs it nothing.
- **Stricter versions of the escape.** A legal step beyond the free reach of every enemy head on the board, not
  only the rammer: 15 of 27 main, 57 of 75 v31. A legal step beyond free steps plus the rammer's whole length less
  one (my reading of the most a sprint can pay for): 4 of 27 main, 25 of 75 v31. She had a single legal step in 12
  of 27 main rams and none in 1.
- **Alone against her own base rate.** Over all turns of our queen while theirs is dead (main, 8746 turns) she has
  fewer than two friends within 5 in 0.581, and in 0.635 in the 30 games with a ram. At the ram it is 27 of 30
  (binomial tail against 0.581: p 0.0002, turns are not independent so read it as a direction). Rammed while their
  queen was still alive (56 rams): alone in 0.732. So rammed queens are lonelier than usual, lone period or not.
- **Same for their queen.** Their lone queen, main: alone 35 of 42 against a base rate of 0.466; step beyond free
  25 of 41; beyond actual 13 of 41. Ours against theirs, Fisher: alone p 0.51, beyond free p 1.0, beyond actual
  p 0.61. On v31 our queen had the step more often than theirs (59 of 75 against 33 of 57, p 0.013; beyond actual
  43 of 75 against 16 of 57, p 0.001), which again says their rammers pay for the extra steps more often than ours
  (43 of 75 against 26 of 58).
- **How often.** Rams per 1000 lone-queen turns: ours 3.4, theirs 2.8 on main; 5.3 against 2.0 on v31.
- **Chance.** Wilson 95 percent: 27 of 30 is 0.74 to 0.97; 17 of 27 is 0.44 to 0.78; 11 of 27 is 0.25 to 0.59;
  v31 57 of 75 is 0.65 to 0.84, 59 of 75 is 0.68 to 0.86, 43 of 75 is 0.46 to 0.68. Main against v31 for the step
  beyond free: Fisher p 0.13. Without three supporting rams: 24 of 27 alone, 14 of 24 beyond free, 8 of 24 beyond
  actual.
- **One opponent, layout, evening.** Main: 18 opponents (largest 4 rams), 12 layouts, 2 evenings. Leave one out:
  alone 0.875 to 0.931; beyond free 0.571 to 0.696 by layout and 0.600 to 0.680 by opponent. Default carries the
  strict count: 6 rams there, 5 with a step beyond the actual distance; without Default it is 6 of 21. v31 by
  evening: beyond free 44 of 48 on the first evening and 15 of 27 on the second, so that share is not steady;
  under 1700 it is 1 of 6.
- **Who is involved.** Our queen was 2 or 3 long in 27 of the 30 main rams. Two of the 30 happened with no other
  dragon of ours alive and 8 with fewer than five, so "alone" is partly "nobody left" in a quarter of them.
