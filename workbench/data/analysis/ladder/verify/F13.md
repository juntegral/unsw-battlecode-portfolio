# F13: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every count reproduces exactly in all five rows. On main a legal single step that ends beyond
the full reach of every enemy head within 6 tiles and of the rammer existed in 5 of 28 rams on our lone queen
(Wilson 0.08 to 0.36); beyond the rammer alone in 9 of 28. In 14 of the 28 she had one legal step or none, so
there was nothing to choose. It is spread over 18 opponents, 13 layouts and two half-days. Three things belong
next to it. (1) The measure is the strict one: a step that no nearby enemy could reach even by paying its whole
body. Against what the rammer really did (a step farther than the distance it covered and beyond its free steps)
there were 11 of 28, and beyond free steps alone 15 to 17. So "rarely" is 0.18 on the strict reading and about
0.4 on the lenient one. (2) It is a main result: on v31 the strict count is 33 of 75 (0.44); main against v31
Fisher p 0.02. (3) On main it is no different for their lone queen rammed by ours: 7 of 42 (p 1.0).

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own rebuild; board at the turnStart of her last own turn; path distance by
  breadth-first search over my own graph (kelp closed, portals followed, wrap, dragons ignored); a legal step is a
  neighbour over that graph with no body tile on it. Compared with rows.jsonl only afterwards (no difference in
  685 games on code, result, last round, queen death round and cause).
- **Is ceil(L/4) + L - 2 the real limit?** Over all 555 rams on a queen (both codes, all layouts) the rammer made
  more steps than that in 17, and in all 17 it had grown on the way (it ate pearls during the sprint). So the
  formula is the limit on an empty path, and pearls on the path stretch it in 3 percent of rams. With one more
  paid step (L - 1) the strict count is 2 of 28 on main and 22 of 75 on v31.
- **What blocks her on main.** No step beyond the rammer's own full reach: 19. Beyond the rammer but inside
  another head's reach: 4. Safe: 5. Counting only heads within 4, 3 or 2 tiles instead of 6: 5, 5, 6.
- **How much choice she had.** Legal steps: none in 1 ram, one in 13, two in 6, three in 8. She was 2 long in 17
  of the 28 and 3 long in 5. The rammer was 2 or 3 long in 20 and at path distance 2 or 3 from her in 20; it was
  within 6 tiles at her last turn in 26.
- **What rammers did.** Steps above their free steps in the ram turn: none in 12, one in 10, two in 4, three in 2.
  So 16 of 28 paid length to reach her, which is why the free-reach count (15) says little.
- **The middle reading.** A legal step farther from the rammer than max(its free steps, the distance it really
  covered): main 11 of 28 (0.39, Wilson 0.24 to 0.58), v31 43 of 75; their lone queen 13 of 42 and 16 of 57.
- **Ours against theirs, main against v31** (strict count, Fisher). Main ours 5 of 28 against theirs 7 of 42: p
  1.0. v31 ours 33 of 75 against theirs 11 of 57: p 0.003. Our lone queen, main against v31: p 0.021. Lone or not
  makes no difference on main (12 of 58 with their queen alive).
- **One opponent, layout, evening.** Main: 18 opponents (largest 4 rams), 13 layouts; by half-day 2 of 16 and 3 of
  12; by band 2 of 8, 2 of 17, 1 of 3. Two of the five safe ones are on Default (2 of 6 there). v31 by half-day:
  24 of 48 and 9 of 27; by band 1 of 6, 14 of 34, 11 of 25, 7 of 10.
- **Without three supporting rams.** 5 of 25.
- **Not checked.** The base rate: how often a lone queen has such a step on turns when nobody rams her. Without
  it the count says the rammed positions had no sure way out, not that they were tighter than her usual ones.
