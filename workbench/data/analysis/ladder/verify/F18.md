# F18: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every number reproduces exactly. On Stripes, in rounds 0-199, when the only adjacent pearls
lie through a portal in a pocket, our dragons end the turn without eating in 0.906 of 385 chances on main (0.867 of
1175 on v31) and opponents in 0.395 of 301 (0.375 of 856). Our rate is the higher one in 8 of 9 main games and 23
of 25 v31 games, at both rating levels, and no opponent or game carries it. On the other layouts together we pass
up less than opponents do (0.197 against 0.243 for all chances, 0.273 against 0.385 for pocket chances). Three
things to read with it. (1) The pockets are cells of 1, 3 or 4 tiles, and the dragon that takes the pearl is dead
within 10 rounds in about two cases out of three on either side (ours 23 of 36, theirs 134 of 182 on main),
mostly exactly four rounds later against a wall. Passing up is declining a dead end; the count does not show that
taking would pay. (2) Per dragon the gap is smaller than per turn: half of our dragons that had such a chance did
take one at some point (31 of 60; theirs 158 of 178), and a few dragons that wait beside a portal supply many of
our chances. (3) "Not on other layouts" is true pooled, but on Prisoners Dilemma #2, Schooltime #1 and Trauma we
pass up more than opponents in both codes, at a much lower level (about 0.2 against 0.1).

- **What the pocket is.** The pearl tiles behind the portal lie in regions of 1, 3 or 4 tiles (main, ours: 91,
  179, 115 tile-chances). The dragon is 2 or 3 long in all but a handful of chances. Fate of the dragon within 10
  rounds, main: after taking, ours dead in 23 of 36 (0.64), theirs in 134 of 182 (0.74); after passing up, ours
  0.112 of 349, theirs 0.202 of 119. v31: after taking 88 of 156 (0.56) and 370 of 535 (0.69); after passing up
  0.100 and 0.227. Of their 134 main deaths 96 come exactly four rounds after the meal; causes wall 98, no action
  25, own body 7, head-on 4 (v31: 252 of 370 at four rounds; wall 186, no action 141). Ours: wall 16, own body 6,
  head-on 1. So the usual outcome of taking is one pearl of growth and then death inside the cell, where the body
  drops new pearls that are again pocket pearls behind a portal.
- **Passing up and the result.** v31: our rate is 0.762 of 581 in the 9 games we won and 0.970 of 594 in the 17 we
  lost; main 0.876 of 113 (2 wins) and 0.919 of 272 (7 losses). Their rate is 0.33 to 0.38 in the games they won.
