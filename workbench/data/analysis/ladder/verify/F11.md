# F11: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every number reproduces exactly. On the five large layouts, among natural pearls born within 6
tiles of a head of both sides, ours is the nearer head in 0.412 of the non-tied births on main and 0.401 on v31
(theirs in 0.59 and 0.60), and of the pearls they are nearer to they eat 0.882 (0.906 on v31) while we eat 0.774
(0.764) of ours. Both halves hold on each of the five layouts and in both rating bands, they are far outside
chance by game and by layout, and two plainer explanations fail: it is not a head-count effect (with head counts
within 10 percent the share is 0.423 and 0.406 and the eating gap stays) and it is not that their pearls are
simply closer (matched on how near and how much nearer, we eat 0.78 and they eat 0.88 to 0.90). Limits: the eating
gap is small against 1700-1899 on main (0.054), "about 6 of 10" leaves out the ties (11 percent of births), and
two small layouts (Stripes, Prisoners Dilemma #2) show the same pattern, so it is not only a large-layout fact.
