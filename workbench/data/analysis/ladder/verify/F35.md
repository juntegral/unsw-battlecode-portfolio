# F35: lone queens (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 08:23 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.**

The counts are right. Every number in the queue comes out the same from the raw replays (full games on free
layouts: main ours 8 of 43, theirs 32 of 80, ratio 0.47; v31 8 of 67 against 36 of 107, ratio 0.35), the gap is
larger than chance, and no single opponent, layout or evening carries it. What does not hold is the reading that a
lone queen of ours stays alive less well in general on main. The first part only looks at games that go the full
500 rounds, and that leaves out the games where a side was wiped out, which we do to them about twice as often as
they do to us (main 76 against 36). 30 of our 38 surviving lone queens on main are in those wins. With every game
counted the two sides are level: main ours 38 of 102, theirs 45 of 130 (ratio 1.08); v31 47 of 159 against 61 of
182 (ratio 0.88). The second part (deaths per lone turn, all games) holds on v31: 7.87 against 4.11 per 1,000,
ratio 1.92, interval 1.48 to 2.48, the same in every slice. It does not hold on main: over all 255 free main games
it is 5.32 against 4.33, ratio 1.23, interval 0.89 to 1.70, and 1.06 once games are grouped by who is ahead at
r100. What does hold on both codes is narrower: in games level in total length at r100, early endings included,
our lone queen dies about three times as fast as theirs (main 9.41 against 3.05 per 1,000 lone turns, v31 8.53
against 3.25) and is alive at the end in 2 of 21 and 2 of 32 cases against 11 of 30 and 15 of 44.

## Checks

**Chance, first part.** Fisher p 0.017 on main, 0.0012 on v31, 0.0001 pooled. Resampling games, the main ratio
runs from 0.20 to 0.85 and is at or above 0.7 in 10 percent of resamples (at or above 1 in under 1 percent); v31
0.14 to 0.64, at or above 0.7 in under 1 percent. Resampling opponents instead: main 0.09 to 0.85 (10 percent at
or above 0.7), v31 0.04 to 0.84 (7 percent). So "lower" is safe on both codes; "less than 0.7" is safe on v31 and
pooled, and about nine to one on main alone. The band slices are too small to stand alone: main 1700-1899 is
4 of 18 against 10 of 31, p 0.53.

**One opponent, layout or evening.** No. Their 32 surviving lone queens on main come from 19 opponents;
the largest are 4 of 11, 4 of 5 and 3 of 4. Without the top two opponents the ratio is 0.53; leaving out any one
opponent it stays between 0.32 and 0.51. By layout the largest are Around UNSW 6 of 12, Maze 5 of 13, Tower
Defense 5 of 8, Portals 4 of 8; without the top two it is 0.52, leaving out any one layout 0.38 to 0.58, and our
share is the lower one on 8 of the 10 layouts that have lone queens on both sides. v31: without the top two
opponents 0.42 (any one out: 0.15 to 0.39), without the top two layouts 0.57 (any one out: 0.28 to 0.45).
Evenings: main 10-06 7 of 38 against 29 of 70, 10-07 1 of 5 against 3 of 10; v31 10-05 2 of 21 against 14 of 44,
10-06 6 of 46 against 22 of 63.

**Three most supporting games removed.** Worst case main 0.50 (three games where our lone queen died), v31 0.38
(three where theirs lived).
