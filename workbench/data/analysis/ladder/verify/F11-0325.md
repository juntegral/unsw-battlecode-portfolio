# F11: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every number reproduces exactly: on the five large layouts our share of pearls eaten in raw
rounds 101-300 is 0.334 on main and 0.341 on v31, against 0.471 and 0.473 on the other layouts, and the gap is
there at 1700-1899 and 1900-2099 in both codes. It is not carried by an opponent, an evening or a few games, and
it is not a head-count effect: on the large layouts we still hold 48 percent of the living dragons after r100 on
main (49 on v31) while eating a third of the pearls. Three things narrow the reading. The "other layouts" figure
of 0.47 is an average over layouts that run from 0.09 to 0.78, and two small layouts (Tower Defense, Stripes) are
worse than any large one. Counted by layout instead of by game, five layouts chosen at random out of the 19 do
this badly in about 5 percent of draws. And the window is not special: the same layouts are already at 0.43 in
rounds 0-100 and stay at 0.33 after round 300.
