# F24: holds in part

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count reproduces. What holds: on main the share of games in which our queen is
rammed rises step by step with the opponent's rating (0.33 at 1700-1799, 0.38, 0.41, 0.62, 0.63, 0.80 at 2300 and
over; shuffling the ratings between battles reaches the trend in 6 of 4000), and against teams rated 2100 or more
our queen is alive at the end in 0 of 13 games on main and 2 of 43 on v31. What does not hold as worded: on v31 the
rammed share does not rise with rating. It is 0.62 under 1700, 0.51 at 1700-1799, 0.32 at 1800-1899, and between
0.53 and 0.67 in every band from 1900 up. The three-band rise in the queue (0.39, 0.54, 0.60) is one low cell,
1800-1899 (118 games, 27 battles, 13 opponents); with those battles left out the shuffles reach the trend in 650
of 4000. And main's 2100+ cell is thin: 13 games, 3 battles, 2 opponents; it cannot be told from the 1900-2099
band (rammed p 0.24, alive p 0.13). On v31 the 2100+ cell is broad (8 opponents, 10 battles).
