# F31, verifier run of 2026-10-07 07:35 KST

## Verdict: holds in part

**What holds.** The step is real, it is ours alone, and it is tied to the round. On the six layouts above 1200 cells
our non-queen dragons leave a free pearl that lies next to their head on 0.24 of such turns in raw rounds 126-150 and
on 0.40 in rounds 151-175 (main; v31 0.23 and 0.41). The opponents stay between 0.21 and 0.25 throughout. The change
falls between raw round 150 and raw round 151, within one round, in both codes, in both rating bands, before and
after the 6 October cut, and against 27 of 29 (main) and 39 of 39 (v31) opponents. Our pearls and our splits fall
with it. On the smaller layouts there is no step at 151 and the same step falls between raw rounds 300 and 301. The
dragon limit does not produce it: the fall is just as clear in games that never came near 60 dragons. The
analyst's numbers recount exactly from my own rebuild of the raw replays, with the two exceptions named below.

**What does not hold as worded.**
1. Schooltime #1 (one of the six big layouts) does not show the step at 151 in the adjacent-pearl rate: 0.33 of 528 turns
   before, 0.37 of 558 after, both codes together (the other five rise by 0.08 to 0.25; the sample is
   small, so a small step is not excluded). Our pearls there rise across
   round 151 (ratio 1.27 and 1.28), only less than theirs; our splits do fall (0.22 and 0.47 on 7 and 5 games).
2. The step at 301 cannot be seen on Devil or Stripes: only 5 of 44 Devil games and no Stripes game run long enough.
   On Trophy and the two Prisoners Dilemma layouts it rests on 24, 8 and 5 turns in rounds 301-325. It is clear on the other eight.
3. The data do not fix the boundary at 1200 cells. The largest layout with the late step has 1152 cells (Maze,
   Trauma) and the smallest with the early step has 1701 (Slithery Fight); any cut from 1153 to 1700 fits, and so
   does a cut on width (the six are 56 to 64 wide, the others 54 or narrower).

**Two of the analyst's numbers were counted on another game set than the queue describes** (splits at round 300, and
the cost bar); the conclusion is the same either way. Details below. **One new result on cost** is in check 7.

### 7. Does the result data show a cost?

Not in wins on the big layouts; one signal on the other layouts; a trade in the state numbers on both.

- **The analyst's bar, big layouts:** lower halves (stronger fall in our pearls) won 44 of 95, upper halves 38 of 95,
  Fisher p = 0.46. Nothing.
- **The same bar at round 300 on the other layouts** (our pearls r301-400 / r201-300, median within layout and
  code, games that reached round 400 with 10 or more of ours after round 300): lower halves won 40 of 89, upper
  halves 62 of 89, Fisher p = 0.0014. This is the only result-level signal I found. It is not proof of cost: a side
  that is losing after round 300 eats less for that reason alone, and the bar cannot tell the two apart. The big
  layouts, where the same confusion would apply, show nothing.
- **Shares of the board.** Big layouts, games reaching round 300: our share of all dragon length is 0.479 after
  round 150 and 0.385 after round 300 (main), 0.475 and 0.367 (v31); our share of living dragons 0.487 and 0.400,
  0.481 and 0.400. Other layouts, games reaching round 450: length share 0.466 after round 300 and 0.457 after 450
  (main), 0.476 and 0.440 (v31); dragons 0.479 and 0.425, 0.496 and 0.472. Before their step the other layouts are
  flat (main 0.460, 0.461, 0.470, 0.463, 0.457 after rounds 100, 150, 200, 250, 300). On the big layouts the slide
  starts at the step (main 0.486, 0.480, 0.449, 0.415, 0.391 after the same rounds).
- **Our share of pearls eaten** drops at the step by construction: big 0.435 (r101-150) to 0.344 (r151-200) in main,
  0.432 to 0.339 in v31; other layouts 0.481 (r201-300) to 0.418 (r301-400), 0.510 to 0.461.
- **The longest dragon moves the other way, on both groups.** Big: ours longer against theirs longer 29 against 48
  after round 150 and 47 against 36 after round 300 (main); 47 against 58 and 60 against 54 (v31). Other layouts: 36
  against 39 after round 300 and 62 against 31 after round 450 (main); 36 against 61 and 67 against 58 (v31).

So after the step we give up pearls, dragons and total length, and our longest dragon gains on theirs. Whether that
trade wins or loses games cannot be read from these games, because every build of ours has the step. Win rates (main
35 of 88 big against 122 of 192 other; v31 48 of 119 against 155 of 291) compare different layouts and are not a
measure of it.
