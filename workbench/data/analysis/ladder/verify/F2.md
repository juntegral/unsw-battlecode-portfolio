# F2: of head-on drop pearls eaten within 15 rounds we eat about 40%, in every slice

**Verdict: holds in part.**

Every count comes out the same from the raw replays. The 40 / 60 split holds for all main games, for 1900-2099 and
for both halves. It does not hold in the 1700-1899 slice, so "in every slice" is wrong: there we have 45.5%
(battle-resampled 95% interval 40.7 to 50.4), we were credited fewer than them in 30 games and more in 28, and
without that slice's three most supporting games it is 47.9%. That slice is close to even, not 40 / 60.

Two things to know before using the number:

1. **It is not special to head-on meetings.** Our share of every pearl eaten in these 195 games is 41.3% (natural
   pearls 42.2%, pearls from any death 40.2%). The head-on figure is the same split as the whole pearl economy, so
   by itself it does not show that we lose the scramble after a head-on.
2. **It is a pooled figure with opposite halves inside.** The side that made the move takes most of the pearls:
   when our dragon was the mover we took 58.7%, when theirs was we took 29.8%, and theirs was the mover in 5,090 of
   7,787 meetings (65%). By layout the share runs from 24-26% (Schooltime #1, Islands, Australia) to 53-56% (Devil,
   Autarky, Default), and five large layouts supply two thirds of the pearls counted.

## Checks

- **My count is independent.** Own state rebuild (see F7.md). A meeting is two reason-3 deaths in one turn with one
  dragon from each team (739 same-team meetings left out); drop pearls are the pearls that appear right after each
  death, all on even segments, and their number equals ceil(length / 2) for every death in every game. Meeting
  count, drop totals and the within-15 credits agree with rows.jsonl in all 195 games.
- **Chance.** All games: 119 fewer against 68 more, sign test p = 0.0002. 1900-2099: 81 against 39. 1700-1899: 30
  against 28, which is no difference.
- **Three most supporting games removed.** All: 41.4% (the three are Australia 35 v 388, Islands 65 v 312,
  Australia 54 v 288). 1900-2099: 39.7%. First half 41.0%, second half 43.3%. 1700-1899: 47.9%.
- **One layout?** No for the pooled number: leaving one out gives 39.3% (without Default) to 41.9% (without
  Islands). But the layouts differ a lot (games, us, them, share, games fewer / more):
  Around UNSW 14, 2649, 3601, 42%, 12 / 2; Slithery Fight 12, 1618, 2136, 43%, 11 / 1; Schooltime #2 6, 1201, 1514,
  44%, 4 / 2; Islands 8, 696, 2079, 25%, 8 / 0; Australia 9, 725, 2036, 26%, 8 / 1; Maze 15, 640, 1081, 37%, 15 / 0;
  Schooltime #1 9, 251, 812, 24%, 9 / 0; Trauma 18, 227, 507, 31%, 17 / 1; Default 15, 919, 731, 56%, 3 / 12;
  Devil 13, 698, 625, 53%, 3 / 10; Autarky 8, 553, 460, 55%, 1 / 7; Queen Of Spades 17, 277, 314, 47%, 7 / 10;
  weakhold 9, 232, 249, 48%, 5 / 4; Tower Defense 13, 118, 146, 45%, 6 / 7; Trophy 6, 212, 178, 54%, 2 / 4;
  Stripes 8, 25, 53, 32%, 5 / 1; Prisoners Dilemma #1 3, 64, 37; #2 6, 61, 28; Portals 6 games, no meetings.
- **One opponent?** No. Leaving one out gives 39.2% to 40.9%. Per opponent 27% (Settlers of Battlecode) to 57%
  (POFPOF); we are above 50% against 2 of 22.
- **One evening?** All main games are from one evening, so not testable. By bot id: 18504 40.6%, 18570 39.3%,
  18066 39.4%.
- **Why 1700-1899 differs.** Not one layout: leaving one out inside that slice gives 44.0% to 47.0%. On the layouts
  both slices played, the lower band is higher on most (Default 65% v 50%, Devil 66% v 51%, Autarky 63% v 49%,
  Australia 40% v 19%, Schooltime #2 50% v 34%).
- **Who dropped.** Pearls dropped by our dragons (14,855): we ate 5,787, they 7,383 (43.9%). Dropped by theirs
  (16,446): we ate 5,379, they 9,204 (36.9%).
- **Who moved.** Our dragon was the mover in 2,697 meetings: us 5,876, them 4,134 (58.7%). Theirs in 5,090: us
  5,290, them 12,453 (29.8%). The mover's side wins the pearls in both cases; they make the move about twice as
  often and keep a larger part when they do (70% against our 59%).
- **Numbers on the board.** If the pearls were split like living dragons at the moment of the meeting we would have
  49.6%. Meetings with similar counts (0.8 to 1.25): 42.2%. Where we outnumber them: 55.3%. Where they outnumber us:
  24.9%. Our share of dragon turns over the whole games is 46.7%. So against our head count we are about 5 to 9
  points short, on drop pearls and on natural pearls alike.
- **When.** Meetings in r0-100: 48.1%. r101-300: 39.3%. Later: 39.1%.
- **Result.** Games we won (111): 47.7%. Games we lost (84): 31.5%. Even in won games we are below half.
