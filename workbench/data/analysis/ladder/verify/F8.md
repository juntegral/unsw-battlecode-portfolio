# F8: holds

**Verdict: holds.** Every count reproduces exactly. In cross-team head-on meetings the opponent's dragon is the
mover in 0.657 of 8313 meetings on main and 0.675 of 16491 on v31, between 0.63 and 0.72 in each main rating
band and time half, and above one half against 22 of the 24 opponents with five or more main games. We lose less
length in these meetings than they do (28067 against 31212, ratio 0.899, game bootstrap 0.86 to 0.94). It is not
a head-count effect: after r100 we have 49 percent of the living dragons on main and 51 on v31. The qualifier is
the layout: the share runs from about one third to 0.82 by layout, the five large layouts and Slithery Fight
hold 61 percent of the meetings and all sit at 0.65 or more, and on five small layouts (both Prisoners Dilemma,
Autarky, Devil, weakhold) it is at or under one half on main. "Two thirds" is a pooled figure that leans on the
big maps, not a constant.

  our heads about twice as readily as ours step into theirs.
