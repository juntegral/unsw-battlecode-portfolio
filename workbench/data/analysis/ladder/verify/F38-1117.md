# F38: long stays of the queen on narrow tiles (verifier, run of 2026-10-07 11:17 KST)

**Verdict: holds in part.** Every count in the queue recounts exactly. On main the ratio is real in every slice asked
for. Its size is loose, it lives on three layouts and a few games, and the last sentence ("it is not our own dragons
that hold her there") is right about the stays and misses how half of them end.

What holds: on main, of the stays begun in r101-200, 117 of 538 of ours are long against 18 of 485 of theirs (5.9
times). It is there in both halves and both rating bands, 0 of 10,000 side swaps by game give a gap as large, with
the layout mix held equal it is 5.1 times, and it is not sprinting (runs of three or more narrow tiles with every
step counted: 110 of 546 against 17 of 514). Per turn inside long stays the queens die equally often on main over all
rounds (23.7 against 24.1 per 1,000). Own dragons are beside her in 20 of the 192 long stays.

What limits it:

1. **Size and carriers.** 108 of the 117 long stays are on Maze, weakhold and Tower Defense, and they sit in 33 of
   the 293 games. Three games hold 42 of them; two Tower Defense games against NUSW Battlecode hold 30 of the 37 on
   that layout, and on v31 Tower Defense runs the other way (2 of 23 against 11 of 29). Battle bootstrap of the
   ratio: 3.0 to 13.6. Without the three games 4.5 times; without the three layouts 9 of 236 against 4 of 317.
   Half 1 rests on one long stay of theirs (16 of 174 against 1 of 101). So "about five times" is somewhere between
   three and fourteen. On Maze our share is the same on v31 and main (41 of 152, 46 of 176); what changed there is
   their queens (16 of 63, then 1 of 41).
2. **"Die equally often per turn."** Right on main over all rounds, with a wide interval (ratio 0.98, 0.60 to 1.61).
   In the window of the claim it rests on 3 deaths of theirs on main (16 in 569 turn starts against 3 in 109); with
   both codes it is 38 in 942 against 8 in 317, 1.6 times (0.75 to 3.4). How they die differs: of our 45 deaths in
   long stays on main 30 are wall or own body, of their 24, 12.
3. **Own dragons.** 20 of 192 is exact, so they are not why she is there for long. But those 20 stays are 19 of her
   38 deaths in long stays (theirs 0 of 8). Read from the replays: in 17 of the 19 the own dragon is first beside
   her at the turn start she dies in, in 19 of 19 no tile next to her head is free at that turn start, and it is a
   child of hers in 5 (split off during that stay in 3). Over all rounds and stays of any length, both codes, 80
   deaths of our queen come this way (of 496) against 28 of theirs (of 486). One of our dragons takes her last free
   tile in a corridor.
